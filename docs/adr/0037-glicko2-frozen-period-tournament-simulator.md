# ADR 0037: Validated Glicko-2 `serde` Primitives, Frozen-Period Tournament Kernels, and Client-Side Tournament Simulator

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
[`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/mod.rs) previously implemented Mark Glickman's Illinois volatility root-finder and single-competitor `update_rating` step, while [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/mod.rs) exposed only evolutionary `ReplicatorDynamicsTool`. Supporting interactive multi-competitor Bayesian tournament modeling across native desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` revealed three gaps:
1. **Unserialized Core Glicko-2 Newtypes**: `Rating`, `RatingDeviation`, `Volatility`, `SystemConstant`, `GlickoPlayer`, and `MatchResult` in [`core.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/core.rs) lacked `serde::Serialize` and validated `serde::Deserialize` implementations, preventing boundary-enforced JSON preset persistence and match-log deserialization.
2. **Order-Dependent Sequential Updates vs. Canonical Frozen Rating Periods**: Applying `update_rating` sequentially in-place as matches arrive mutates intermediate opponent ratings $(\mu_j, \phi_j)$ within the same round, violating Glickman's canonical simultaneous rating-period specification, omitting zero-match inactivity deviation inflation ($\phi^* = \sqrt{\phi^2 + \sigma^2}$), and lacking deterministic Swiss / Round-Robin pairing generators or zero-allocation hot-path evaluation under `VerifiedAllocator`.
3. **Interactive Client-Side Tournament Analysis and Bounded Sharing**: Users lacked an offline-capable GUI tool in `GameTheoryTab` to inspect $r \pm 2\,\text{RD}$ 95% confidence bands, $\sigma$ volatility shifts, pairwise head-to-head win matrices, and frozen-vs-sequential drift, or to atomically import/export CSV and JSON match logs, standings, and pairings while respecting the 4,096-byte `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` share-link cap.

## Decision
1. **Validated `serde` Serialization on Core Glicko-2 Types**:
   - Implement `Serialize` and custom/validated `Deserialize` across `Rating`, `RatingDeviation`, `Volatility`, `SystemConstant`, `GlickoPlayer`, and `MatchResult` in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/core.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/core.rs) (with `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` on structs), routing every deserialized scalar through `Rating::new`, `RatingDeviation::new`, `Volatility::new`, `SystemConstant::new`, and `MatchResult::new` so non-finite or out-of-range payloads fail at the deserialization boundary.
   - Extend `Glicko2Error` in [`crates/pure_math/src/error.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/error.rs) with `PlayerIndexOutOfBounds`, `SelfMatch`, `InsufficientPlayers`, and `DimensionMismatch` while preserving full backwards compatibility for existing `update_rating` callers.
2. **Frozen-Period Kernels, Deterministic Pairings, and Seeded Simulation**:
   - Add [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/tournament.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/tournament.rs) and re-export its API in [`glicko2/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/mod.rs), providing `PeriodMatch`, `Pairing`, `SwissRoundPlan`, zero-allocation `evaluate_rating_period_into` (verified under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`), canonical order-invariant `evaluate_rating_period`, comparative `evaluate_sequential_period`, `head_to_head_win_probability`, `pairwise_win_matrix` (`DMatrix<f64>`), circle-method `round_robin_schedule` (even and odd rosters), rematch-avoiding `swiss_pairings` with odd-roster bye rotation, and `simulate_period_matches` seeded strictly via `OxidizeRng`.
3. **Client-Side `Glicko2TournamentTool`, Atomic Web/Desktop I/O, and URL Budget Warnings**:
   - Register `Glicko2TournamentTool` (`"Glicko-2 Tournament"`) in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/mod.rs) split across [`glicko2.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/glicko2.rs), [`glicko2/ui.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/glicko2/ui.rs), and [`glicko2/io.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/glicko2/io.rs), backed by `Glicko2TournamentPresetConfig` in [`presets.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/presets.rs) and [`presets/glicko2.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/presets/glicko2.rs) plus two starter presets (`glicko2_swiss_upset`, `glicko2_round_robin_drift`) in [`starter_gallery.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/starter_gallery.rs).
   - Wire `spawn_open_file_dialog`, `spawn_save_as_file_dialog`, and `strip_utf8_bom` from [`web_io.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/web_io.rs) for atomic CSV/JSON match-log import and `.csv`/`.json` standings and pairings export, and surface a proactive URL payload budget warning whenever local rosters exceed `MAX_DECODED_URL_STATE_BYTES` (`4,096` bytes) while keeping local exports unrestricted.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Multi-competitor Glicko-2 rating periods are bit-exact invariant to match order, apply exact inactivity RD growth, execute with zero heap allocations on preallocated buffers, and run 100% client-side on native desktop and WebAssembly without external rating APIs.
- **Positive**: Malformed scores, self-matches, and unknown competitors in imported CSV/JSON logs are rejected atomically without mutating active GUI state, and starter presets round-trip losslessly within the 4 KiB URL share envelope.
- **Neutral**: Public additions to `pure_math::statistics::glicko2` and `pure_math::error::Glicko2Error` are recorded in [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json), and GUI tool/preset registration counts are updated in the contract test suites.
