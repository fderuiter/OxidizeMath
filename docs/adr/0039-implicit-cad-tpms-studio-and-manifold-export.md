# ADR 0039: Implicit CAD & TPMS Studio, Boundary-Sealed Marching Cubes, and Millimeter Manifold Export

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0004](0004-unified-verification-dependency-audit.md), [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), and [ADR 0038](0038-watertight-3d-stl-and-ply-exports.md))

## Context
OxidizeMath already included symbolic AST parsing in [`crates/math_commons/src/ast/mod.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/mod.rs), Marching Cubes extraction in [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/mod.rs), parametric differential geometry in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs), and Wavefront `.obj` serialization in [`crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export.rs`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export.rs). Four gaps prevented browser and desktop users from authoring watertight, 3D-printable millimeter meshes directly inside `math_explorer_gui`:
1. **2D-Only Hot-Loop AST Evaluation and Missing CSG Operators**: `Expr` lacked `min(a, b)` and `max(a, b)` operators for Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) unions, intersections, and differences, and lacked a zero-allocation `eval_3d(x, y, z)` evaluator for dense $N^3$ voxel grids.
2. **Unsealed Isosurfaces, Unscaled Voxel Gradients, and Per-Extraction Heap Allocations**: Periodic TPMS fields such as Gyroid and Schwarz P clipped at a finite bounding box leave open boundary edges unless exterior samples are clamped positive (`f > isovalue`). In addition, `CentralDifferenceEstimator` computed gradients in unit-index space rather than millimeter space, `MarchingCubes::extract` allocated fresh `TriangleMesh` and `SliceEdgeCache` buffers on every slider drag, and triangle winding followed inward orientation (`signed_volume_mm3 < 0`).
3. **Open Seams and Pole `NaN` Normals on Parametric Surfaces**: `Sphere` swept $v \in [0, 2\pi]$ (double-covering the sphere) and `SurfaceViewer` emitted unwelded grid vertices with `0.0 / 0.0` `NaN` normals at $v \in \{0, \pi\}$.
4. **No Manifold Diagnostics, Binary `.stl` Serializer, or Browser Binary Mesh Download**: Slicers expect little-endian binary `.stl` files in millimeter units verified for zero open boundary edges, zero `NaN` normals, and positive enclosed volume, downloadable directly from `wasm32-unknown-unknown` and desktop builds.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Allocation 3D AST & CSG Operators**:
   - Extend [`Func`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/expr.rs) with `Func::Min` and `Func::Max` (parsed as two-argument `min(a, b)` and `max(a, b)` calls and formatted in [`crates/math_commons/src/ast/latex.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/latex.rs)) and add `Expr::eval_3d(&self, x: f64, y: f64, z: f64) -> f64` in [`crates/math_commons/src/ast/eval.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/eval.rs), verified under `VerifiedAllocator` to perform zero heap allocations.
2. **Boundary-Sealed, Pre-Allocated Marching Cubes with Outward CCW Winding**:
   - Add optional exterior clamping (`with_exterior_clamp`, `apply_exterior_clamp`, `data_mut`, `resize_dimensions`) to [`VoxelGrid`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/types.rs), scale [`CentralDifferenceEstimator`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/gradients.rs) by physical `grid.voxel_size()` millimeter spacing, and expose reusable [`SliceEdgeCache`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/edge_cache.rs) plus [`MarchingCubes::extract_into`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/isosurface/marching_cubes.rs) emitting outward counter-clockwise triangles (`signed_volume_mm3 > 0`) with zero inner-loop heap allocations.
3. **Manifold Diagnostics, Binary `.stl` Serialization, and Welded Parametric Surfaces**:
   - Add [`MeshDiagnostics`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/analysis.rs) and [`analyze_mesh`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/analysis.rs) alongside [`export_mesh_to_stl_bytes`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export.rs) (`80`-byte header, little-endian `u32` facet count, `50`-byte facets) and cross-target binary download helpers in [`math_explorer_gui/src/web_io.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/web_io.rs).
   - Restrict `Sphere` polar domain to $v \in [0, \pi]$ with analytical pole normals in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs) and weld periodic seams and poles in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/export_utils.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/export_utils.rs) and [`SurfaceViewer`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/surface_viewer.rs).
4. **Interactive `ImplicitCadStudio` and Bookmarkable Starter Presets**:
   - Register [`ImplicitCadStudio`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/implicit_cad/mod.rs) under `#[cfg(feature = "applied")]` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/mod.rs) with `Plot3DViewport` shaded facet projection, shell thickness $|f(x, y, z)| - w/2$, `16..=96` resolution clamping, live manifold diagnostics, millimeter `.stl` / `.obj` downloads, and 3 bookmarkable starter presets (Gyroid, Schwarz P, Torus-Box CSG) within URL fragment bounds.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can design, inspect, share via `#tab=geometry_topology&tool=ImplicitCadStudio&state=...` URLs, and download watertight millimeter `.stl` and `.obj` solids directly in WebAssembly or native builds with zero external Cargo dependencies added.
- **Positive**: Pre-allocated voxel, edge-cache, and mesh buffers keep interactive remeshing allocation-free on inner extraction loops while keeping compressed `.wasm` size well below the `3 MiB` budget.
- **Neutral**: Enabling `seal_boundaries` caps lattice solids smoothly over one outer voxel layer to guarantee manifold slicing (`0` open boundary edges); users can uncheck boundary sealing when inspecting open minimal-surface sheets.
