# ADR 20261124: `#![no_std]` Type-Level SI Exponent Quantities (`math_scalars`), Frame-Separated Constants, and Calibrated Coordinate Boundaries

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 20261123: `#![no_std]` Tier 2 `engine_diffeq` & `engine_pde` Solvers](20261123-nostd-diffeq-and-pde-engines.md), [ADR 20261123: Relativistic Thin-Lens Splat Jacobians](20261123-relativistic-lensed-splats-and-orbits.md), and [ADR 20261123: Implicit CAD Volumetric Studio and Millimeter STL Export](20261123-implicit-cad-capped-isosurface-and-mm-stl.md))

## Context
Physical constant registries, scalar newtypes, astrophysical thin-lens projection, and 3D `.stl` export boundaries previously suffered from five dimensional and coordinate-frame hazards:
1. **Conflicting Bare `f64` Unit Scales in Shared Constant Registries**: [`crates/math_commons/src/constants.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/constants.rs) defined `C = 299_792_458.0` in SI $\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$ alongside `LIGHT_SPEED = 2.997_924_58e23` in nuclear $\text{fm}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$ and `HBAR_C = 197.3` in $\text{MeV}\cdot\text{fm}$, while [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/proton.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/proton.rs) redeclared private bare `f64` `H_BAR` and `K_B` constants.
2. **Type Erasure via `Deref` and Unsound Domain Operations**: `StepSize`, `DiffusionCoeff`, `UnitInterval`, `PositiveFloat`, and `NonNegativeFloat` implemented `Deref<Target = f64>`, silently erasing wrapper boundaries, while domain wrappers (`ElectronVolts`, `Kelvin`, `Cycles`, `Capacity`, `DepthOfDischarge`, and `SurvivalTime`) permitted dimension-erasing `.ln()`, `.exp()`, `.sqrt()`, `.powf()`, self-multiplication (`Self * Self -> Self`), and reciprocal coercion (`f64 / Self -> Self`).
3. **Affine Celsius vs. Absolute Kelvin Mixing**: [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/battery_degradation/model.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/battery_degradation/model.rs) stored bare `f64` Celsius temperatures alongside inline `+ 273.15` Arrhenius conversions and empirical `day^-0.5` factors.
4. **Viewport-to-Kiloparsec Thin-Lens Coordinate Confusion**: [`math_explorer_gui/src/volumetric_scene_lensing.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/volumetric_scene_lensing.rs) passed normalized viewport `halo_scale_radius` and 2D screen offsets directly into `HaloProfile` and `halo_lens_deflection_and_jacobian`, which expect physical kiloparsecs ($\text{kpc}$).
5. **Uncalibrated `UNITS=mm` Binary STL Headers**: [`crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export_stl.rs`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export_stl.rs) stamped `UNITS=mm` into the 80-byte binary `.stl` header of `export_mesh_to_stl_binary` even when serializing unscaled dimensionless meshes.

## Decision
1. **Introduce `#![no_std]` [`crates/00_foundation/math_scalars`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/lib.rs)**:
   - Implement `#[repr(transparent)]` [`Quantity<T, D, F>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/quantity.rs) over generic `libm`-backed [`Scalar`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/scalar.rs) (`f32`, `f64`) on stable Rust `1.99.1` without nightly `generic_const_exprs`.
   - Encode all seven SI base exponents $(L, M, T, I, \Theta, N, J)$ via open canonical Peano-backed signed type-level integers (`Z0`, `PInt<N>`, `NInt<N>`) with [`AddDim`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/dim.rs), `SubDim`, `NegDim`, and even-only `HalfDim` traits, plus disjoint [`UnitFrame`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/frame.rs) tags (`SiFrame`, `NuclearMeVFmFrame`, `GalacticKpcFrame`, `ViewportFrame`, `MillimeterFrame`).
   - Restrict `.ln()`, `.exp()`, `.sin()`, `.cos()`, and `.powf()` exclusively to `Quantity<T, Dimensionless, F>`, and separate affine [`Celsius`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/temperature.rs) from absolute SI `Kelvin` via explicit `Celsius::to_kelvin` conversion.
2. **Partition `math_commons::constants` into `si` and `nuclear` Registries and Harden Wrappers**:
   - Replace bare physical constants in [`crates/math_commons/src/constants.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/constants.rs) with `constants::si` (`SiFrame`) and `constants::nuclear` (`NuclearMeVFmFrame`), wire `mri::proton` and `nuclear::decay::gamow_factor` onto typed `Quantity` arithmetic, and evaluate `ElectrochemicalAgingModel` Arrhenius and $\sqrt{t / t_{\text{ref}}}$ SEI fade via `Celsius`, `Kelvin`, `JoulesPerMole`, and `Seconds`.
   - Remove `Deref`/`DerefMut` from `StepSize`, `DiffusionCoeff`, `UnitInterval`, `PositiveFloat`, and `NonNegativeFloat` in favor of explicit `.get()` / `.value()` accessors, and remove self-multiplication, reciprocal coercion, and dimension-erasing transcendental methods from `solid_state`, `battery_degradation`, and `clinical_trials` domain wrappers.
3. **Enforce Explicit `ViewportFrame` $\leftrightarrow$ `Kiloparsecs` Lensing and `MillimeterMesh` STL Calibration**:
   - Require typed `Kiloparsecs` on astrophysical `HaloProfile` radii and `halo_lensing` / `rotation_curves` solvers, adding `#[serde(default)]` `viewport_kpc_scale` and explicit `viewport_to_kpc` / `kpc_to_viewport` conversions in `volumetric_scene_lensing.rs`.
   - Introduce [`MillimeterMesh`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export_stl.rs) and sealed `StlBinarySource` so `export_mesh_to_stl_binary` stamps `UNITS=mm` exclusively for `[5.0, 250.0] mm` calibrated meshes while raw `Mesh` inputs emit `STL_UNITLESS_HEADER_TEXT` and `SimulationFramework` calibrates unscaled 3D tool meshes before slicer export.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Cross-scale arithmetic (`si::C + nuclear::LIGHT_SPEED`), dimensional transcendentals (`Meters::ln`), odd-exponent square roots, `Cycles * Cycles`, affine `Celsius` Arrhenius misuse, and raw viewport-to-`kpc` assignments fail at compile time with zero runtime memory or allocation overhead (`size_of::<Quantity<f64, ..., SiFrame>>() == 8`).
- **Positive**: Halo critical-curve telemetry aligns with forward splat deflection across viewport scale changes, and binary `.stl` exports claim `UNITS=mm` if and only if coordinates are calibrated in millimeters.
- **Neutral**: Workspace member count increases from 23 to 24 crates, recorded across architecture catalogs, `verification_whitelist.toml`, and `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`.
