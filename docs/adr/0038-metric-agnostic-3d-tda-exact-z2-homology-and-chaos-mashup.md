# ADR 0038: Metric-Agnostic 3D Vietoris-Rips Filtration, Exact Z2 1-Homology, and Chaotic Attractor Mashup

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0020](0020-precompute-distance-matrix-tda-filtration.md) and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
Four architectural limitations prevented interactive topological data analysis on 3D dynamical systems across [`crates/pure_math`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/mod.rs), [`crates/domain_physics`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/mod.rs), and [`math_explorer_gui`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/vietoris_rips/mod.rs):
1. **2D-Locked Public Filtration Signatures**: Although [ADR 0020](0020-precompute-distance-matrix-tda-filtration.md) extracted `vietoris_rips_from_distances` inside [`complex.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/complex.rs), the helper remained `pub(crate)` and [`persistence.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/persistence.rs) accepted only 2D `PointCloud` inputs, blocking 3D Euclidean, Takens delay-coordinate, and custom metric filtrations.
2. **Euler Triangle Over-Subtraction in `betti_number_1`**: [`homology.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/homology.rs) approximated $\beta_1$ via `cycles.saturating_sub(t)` with an `e < v` early exit. In dense cliques such as a $K_4$ 4-clique (where 4 triangles have $\mathbb{Z}_2$ boundary rank 3 because their mod-2 sum vanishes), the 4th dependent triangle erroneously cancelled independent 1-cycles elsewhere in the complex (for example, returning $\beta_1 = 0$ instead of $1$ on $K_4 \sqcup C_4$ and collapsing butterfly wing loops along dense Lorenz trajectory ribbons).
3. **Missing 3D Landmarking & Delay Reconstruction Primitives**: `pure_math::statistics::tda` lacked 3D point cloud containers, deterministic greedy farthest-point subsampling to bound $O(n^3)$ 2-simplex enumeration (`64 choose 3 = 41,664`), Takens 3D delay embeddings $(x(t), x(t - \tau), x(t - 2\tau))$, and preallocated zero-allocation filtration buffers for [`VerifiedAllocator`](../../crates/verified_engine/src/allocator.rs).
4. **Disconnected GUI Chaos & Topology Tools**: [`VietorisRipsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/vietoris_rips/mod.rs) lacked 3D strange attractor filtrations and vertex sculpting, while [`AttractorPlotter`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/attractors.rs) flattened 3D Lorenz trajectories without surfacing [`lorenz_lyapunov`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/metrics.rs) or [`correlation_dimension`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/fractals.rs).

## Decision
1. **Promote Metric-Agnostic Filtration and 3D TDA Primitives in `pure_math::statistics::tda`**:
   - Promote `vietoris_rips_from_distances` in [`complex.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/complex.rs) to `pub`, add `vietoris_rips_complex_3d`, and add `compute_persistence_from_distances` in [`persistence.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/persistence.rs) with `compute_persistence` delegating to it.
   - Introduce [`space3d.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/space3d.rs) providing `Point3D`, `PointCloud3D`, `farthest_point_landmarks`, `farthest_point_landmarks_into`, and `takens_delay_embedding_3d`, re-exported through [`tda/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/mod.rs) and [`math_explorer`](../../math_explorer/src/lib.rs).
2. **Exact $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Boundary Matrix Reduction and Preallocated `TdaFiltrationWorkspace`**:
   - Replace raw triangle subtraction in [`homology.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/homology.rs) with exact $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Gaussian column reduction over packed `u64` edge bitsets: $\beta_1 = (|E| - |V| + \beta_0) - \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}_2}(\partial_2)$.
   - Provide [`workspace.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/tda/workspace.rs) (`TdaFiltrationWorkspace` and `FiltrationStepSummary`) with preallocated flat distance matrices, Union-Find arrays, edge lookup tables, and highest-edge pivot $\mathbb{Z}_2$ bitsets so 24-step 64-landmark persistence sweeps execute with zero heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
3. **Feature-Gated 3D Chaos-Homology Mashup and Tactile Sculpting in `math_explorer_gui`**:
   - Modularize [`VietorisRipsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/vietoris_rips/mod.rs) (`<= 500` lines per file) supporting 2D/N-D clouds up to `MAX_VIETORIS_RIPS_POINTS = 250` and 3D chaotic clouds up to `MAX_CHAOS_LANDMARKS = 64` across `Points2D`, `Points3D`, `Lorenz3D`, and `Takens3D` modes, rendering 3D simplices in [`Plot3DViewport`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/camera/mod.rs) alongside exact $\mathbb{Z}_2$ persistence diagrams/barcodes and tactile 2D/3D `Move`/`Add`/`Erase` brushes while preserving byte-exact legacy 2D preset serialization.
   - Gate all `math_explorer::physics::chaos` calls inside `geometry_topology` behind `#[cfg(feature = "physics")]` so standalone `pure_math` builds compile cleanly, while displaying finite `lorenz_lyapunov` and `correlation_dimension` invariants in both `AttractorPlotter` and `VietorisRipsTool` and enabling one-click 3D landmark handoff from `AttractorPlotter`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `betti_number_1` is algebraically exact over $\mathbb{Z}_2$ on arbitrary 2-skeletons, preserving disjoint 1-cycles alongside dense cliques and locking onto $\beta_1 = 2$ across chaotic 3D Lorenz butterfly wings.
- **Positive**: Preallocated bitset reduction and 64-landmark subsampling keep 24-step persistence sweeps allocation-free on desktop and `wasm32` while respecting strict domain feature isolation (`pure_math` vs `physics`).
- **Neutral**: Computing 3-simplices and $\beta_2$ cavity homology remains intentionally deferred outside this 2-skeleton scope.
