# ADR 0043: Intrinsic `SU(1,1)` Hyperbolic Geometry Engine and Standalone Playground Tab

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0001](0001-dynamic-workspace-discovery.md), [ADR 0008](0008-finite-state-guard-solver-divergence.md), [ADR 0033](0033-wasm32-browser-tests-and-playwright-ci-smoke-gate.md), and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
[`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/mod.rs) previously modeled constant negative curvature exclusively through extrinsic 3D parametric surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^3$ (`Pseudosphere`). Because Hilbert's theorem forbids a complete smooth isometric immersion of the hyperbolic plane $\mathbb{H}^2$ into $\mathbb{R}^3$, the extrinsic pseudosphere terminates at a singular rim and cannot represent global hyperbolic symmetry groups, Fuchsian $\{p, q\}$ tessellations, conformal vs. projective disk projections, or long-horizon Anosov geodesic dynamics. Meanwhile, `GeometryTopologyTab` in [`math_explorer_gui`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/mod.rs) focuses on extrinsic 3D surfaces and topological data analysis, making an isolated, build-discovered hyperbolic playground preferable to coupling unrelated UI controls.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Allocation Intrinsic `SU(1,1)` Hyperbolic Kernel (`pure_math::differential_geometry::hyperbolic`)**:
   - Introduce [`Su11Matrix`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/hyperbolic/mobius.rs) representing $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ \bar{b} & \bar{a} \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SU}(1,1)$ ($|a|^2 - |b|^2 = 1$) with exact composition, inversion, periodic renormalization, tangent direction pushforward, and open-disk coordinate clamping ($|z| \le 1 - 10^{-9}$), alongside conformal Poincaré $\leftrightarrow$ projective Beltrami-Klein maps and Cayley transforms to the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$.
   - Implement [`metrics`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/hyperbolic/metrics.rs) for Poincaré and Klein distances, Liouville intrinsic curvature verification ($K = -1.0$), orthogonal [`GeodesicArc`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/hyperbolic/metrics.rs) sampling into caller-supplied slices, conformal interior angles, and Gauss-Bonnet triangle/polygon area defects.
   - Implement [`HyperbolicTilingBuffer`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/hyperbolic/tiling.rs) for regular $\{p, q\}$ Fuchsian tessellations satisfying $(p - 2)(q - 2) > 4$, pre-allocating bounded tile buffers ($\le 512 \ll 16,384$) and reducing camera matrices modulo edge-midpoint $\pi$-rotations $g_k \in \mathrm{SU}(1,1)$ for drift-free, zero-heap-allocation Möbius panning under `VerifiedAllocator`.
   - Implement [`HyperbolicBilliardSystem`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/hyperbolic/billiards.rs) solving analytical quadratic ray-arc intersections, exact isometric circle-inversion reflections, and unstable-horocycle twin-particle finite-time Lyapunov divergence inside fixed-capacity ring buffers.
2. **Standalone Build-Discovered `HyperbolicGeometryTab` (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Register [`HyperbolicGeometryTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/hyperbolic_geometry/mod.rs) via `// @explorer_feature = "pure_math"` overriding `ExplorerTab::academic_domain` (`"Pure Math"`) and `ExplorerTab::url_slug` (`"hyperbolic_geometry"`) without touching `GeometryTopologyTab`, `app.rs`, or `tabs/mod.rs`.
   - Ship [`HyperbolicDiskTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/hyperbolic_geometry/disk_tool.rs) and [`HyperbolicBilliardsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/hyperbolic_geometry/billiards_tool.rs) with `StarterPresetMetadata` cards, `ToolStateBounds` + `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` URL/preset serialization, `FiniteStateGuard` parameter clamping, WCAG 2.2 keyboard/AccessKit canvas accessibility, and `ExportCapabilities::plot()` CSV/JSON/PNG/SVG/GIF exports.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users and SDK consumers gain a globally complete, allocation-free hyperbolic geometry kernel ($K = -1.0$) with exact $\mathrm{SU}(1,1)$ group closure, Gauss-Bonnet defect rulers, $\{p, q\}$ Fuchsian tilings, and chaotic geodesic billiards verified under both native `VerifiedAllocator` and `wasm32` headless browser tests.
- **Positive**: Auto-discovery isolates all GUI additions inside `math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/hyperbolic_geometry/`, preventing merge conflicts with extrinsic 3D surface and TDA modules.
- **Neutral**: Adding 1 tab, 2 interactive plot tools, and 3 starter presets increments workspace contract counts (`24` tabs, `70` tools, `100` starter presets) and updates `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`.
