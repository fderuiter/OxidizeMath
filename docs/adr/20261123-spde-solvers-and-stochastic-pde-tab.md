# ADR 20261123: Zero-Allocation Stochastic PDE Spectral Solvers, Statistical Mechanics Scaling Telemetry, and StochasticPdeTab

Date: 2026-11-23

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0008](0008-finite-state-guard-solver-divergence.md), [ADR 0021](0021-precompute-stencil-reciprocals.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md), [ADR 20261123](20261123-inventory-tabs-and-scoped-build-scripts.md), and [ADR 20261123](20261123-nostd-diffeq-and-pde-engines.md))

## Context
[`engine_pde`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/lib.rs) and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/mod.rs) provide `#![no_std]` spatio-temporal noise injectors (`spde_noise`), finite-difference SPDE steppers, radix-2 Fourier steppers, and baseline `EdwardsWilkinson1D`/`2D`, `KpzModel1D`/`2D`, `AllenCahn2D`, and `CahnHilliard2D` models. Interactive continuum SPDE exploration in `math_explorer_gui` additionally requires: (1) arbitrary (non-radix-2) 2D periodic spectral Eyre convex-splitting steppers and circularly averaged radial structure-factor binning $S(k, t)$, (2) Lam-Shin conservative $(\nabla h)^2$ + tamed Euler-Maruyama KPZ lattices paired with Family-Vicsek dynamic scaling collapse and log-log $\beta$ regression, (3) temperature-quenched ($T / T_c$) phase-field kinetics with conserved interactive droplet sculpting, and (4) a statically registered `StochasticPdeTab` (`#tab=stochastic_pde`).

## Decision
1. **Arbitrary-Dimension 2D Spectral Stepper and Radial Structure Factor (`pure_math::analysis::pde::spde`)**:
   - Reuse `#![no_std]` [`engine_pde::spde_noise`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/spde_noise.rs) (`inject_additive_noise_1d`, `inject_additive_noise_2d`, `inject_conserved_flux_noise_2d`) and [`SolverWorkspace`](../../crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs) directly rather than introducing a duplicate noise-buffer struct.
   - Add [`SemiImplicitSpectral2D`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/spde/spectral_2d.rs) caching row/column `rustfft` plans, `Complex<f64>` transpose workspaces, and exact 5-point discrete negative-Laplacian symbols $q^2(k_x, k_y)$ for arbitrary grid dimensions (`32..=128`), providing unconditionally energy-stable Eyre convex-splitting reaction-biharmonic and Cahn-Hilliard steppers alongside zero-allocation circularly averaged radial structure-factor binning `compute_radial_structure_factor`.
2. **Lam-Shin KPZ Scaling Telemetry and Quenched Phase-Field Coarsening (`domain_physics::physics::stat_mech`)**:
   - Add [`interface_growth`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/interface_growth/mod.rs) (`InterfaceGrowth1D`, `InterfaceGrowth2D`, `InterfaceScalingTelemetry`) built on [`engine_pde::StencilOperators`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/stencil.rs), `engine_pde::spde_noise`, and [`edwards_wilkinson`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/edwards_wilkinson.rs) (`mean_field`, `interface_roughness`), providing Lam-Shin conservative $(\nabla h)^2$ + tamed Euler-Maruyama stepping, interactive mound deposition, Family-Vicsek data collapse, and OLS log-log $\beta$ regression.
   - Add [`phase_field`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/phase_field/mod.rs) (`PhaseFieldSolver2D`, `PhaseFieldTelemetry`) implementing temperature-quenched ($T / T_c$) Allen-Cahn (Model A) and conserved Cahn-Hilliard (Model B) dynamics via Eyre convex-splitting + `SemiImplicitSpectral2D` + `engine_pde::inject_conserved_flux_noise_2d`, conserved droplet brushing, discrete Ginzburg-Landau free energy $F_h[\phi]$, and radial $S(k, t)$ / $k_{\max}(t)$ / $k_1(t)$ coarsening telemetry.
3. **Static Inventory `StochasticPdeTab` (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Declare `#[cfg(feature = "physics")] pub mod stochastic_pde;` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/mod.rs) and register [`StochasticPdeTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/mod.rs) via `TabDescriptor` (`id = "stochastic_pde"`, `order = 18`) exposing [`KpzInterfaceTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/kpz_tool.rs) and [`SpinodalDecompositionTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/spinodal_tool.rs), four `StarterPresetMetadata` cards, `FiniteStateGuard` rollback, `ToolStateBounds` + `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` validation, conserved zero-mean droplet brushing on Model B, `AccessKit` `Role::GraphicsDocument` canvases, WCAG 2.2 AA colormaps, and deferred PNG/SVG/CSV/JSON exports.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Eliminates all structural duplication with `engine_pde::spde_noise` and `stat_mech::edwards_wilkinson` while preserving zero dynamic heap allocations under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, $\beta_{\text{EW}} \approx 1/4$, $\beta_{\text{KPZ}} \approx 1/3$, strict deterministic Lyapunov free-energy decay $F_h[\phi^{n+1}] \le F_h[\phi^n]$, and Model B mass conservation $|\langle \phi(t) \rangle - \langle \phi(0) \rangle| < 10^{-12}$.
- **Positive**: `StochasticPdeTab` integrates with static `inventory` tab registration and feature-scoped build snapshots while keeping WASM bundle overhead minimal by reusing existing `engine_pde`, `rustfft`, and `math_commons` dependencies.
- **Neutral**: 3D volumetric Cahn-Hilliard grids and coupled hydrodynamic Navier-Stokes (Model H) remain out of scope to preserve interactive 60 FPS desktop and `wasm32` frame budgets.
