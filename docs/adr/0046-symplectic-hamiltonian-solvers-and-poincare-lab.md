# ADR 0046: Zero-Allocation Symplectic Hamiltonian Integrators, Conservative Chaos Models, and Poincaré Section Lab

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0016](0016-ode-multi-solver-comparative-overlays.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
[`crates/domain_physics/README.md`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/README.md) advertises canonical energy conservation checks, double-pendulum chaos, and gravitational N-body orbital dynamics, yet [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/mod.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/mod.rs) and [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/mod.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/mod.rs) previously exposed only dissipative attractors (`LorenzSystem`), 1D logistic maps, and static fractals, while [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/mod.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/mod.rs) contained only static galaxy scaling formulas. Furthermore, [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/solvers.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/solvers.rs) offered only explicit non-symplectic integrators (`Euler`, `Heun`, `RungeKutta4`) whose phase-volume contraction causes monotonic energy loss (`>3%` over `10,000` chaotic double-pendulum steps at `dt = 0.02`), washing out Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser (KAM) invariant tori and periodic celestial choreographies.

## Decision
1. **Canonical Hamiltonian Traits and Stack-Backed Symplectic Integrators (`pure_math`)**:
   - Extend [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/traits.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/traits.rs) with `HamiltonianSystem<State>` (`hamiltonian`, `relative_energy_error`) and `SeparableHamiltonian<State>` (`Coord`, `split_canonical`, `join_canonical`, `grad_kinetic_in_place`, `neg_grad_potential_in_place`).
   - Add [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/implicit_midpoint.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/implicit_midpoint.rs) (`ImplicitMidpoint<State>`, 2nd-order implicit Gauss-Legendre Runge-Kutta) and [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/symplectic.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/symplectic.rs) providing zero-allocation `StormerVerlet<Coord>`, `Yoshida4<Coord>`, and `GaussLegendre4<State>`. Implicit stage fixed-point iterations are strictly capped at `MAX_FIXED_POINT_ITERS = 12` with `try_step_array` finite-state rollback, backed by pre-allocated `ArrayState<N>` scratch buffers, and `ImplicitMidpoint` and `GaussLegendre4` are exposed in `IntegrationMethod` for `OdeSolverTool` (`AnalysisTab`).
2. **Conservative Chaos, 3-Body Choreography, Cubic-Hermite Poincaré Detector, and 4D/6D Lyapunov Metrics (`domain_physics`)**:
   - Expose canonical [`DoublePendulum`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/double_pendulum.rs), separable [`HenonHeiles` / `HenonHeilesSystem`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/henon_heiles.rs), and planar gravitational [`FigureEightThreeBody` / `ThreeBodyChoreography`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/three_body.rs) (`ArrayState<12>` Chenciner-Montgomery choreography with momentum-preserving `perturbed_figure_eight`).
   - Add [`PoincareSection<N>` and `PoincareRingBuffer<CAP>`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/poincare.rs) using safeguarded Newton-bisection on the $O(\Delta t^4)$ cubic Hermite interpolant, plus zero-allocation [`array_lyapunov_exponent`, `lyapunov_exponent_4d`, and `lyapunov_exponent_6d`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/metrics.rs) verified under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
3. **Interactive `HamiltonianChaosLab` and `OdeSolverTool` Symplectic Overlays (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Register [`HamiltonianChaosLab`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/hamiltonian.rs) in `ChaosTab` alongside three `StarterPresetMetadata` cards (`henon_heiles_kam_islands`, `double_pendulum_symplectic_drift`, `figure_eight_three_body`) and wire `ImplicitMidpoint` and `GaussLegendre4` into [`OdeSolverTool`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/ode.rs).
   - Expose pointer click-to-seed and keyboard (`Arrow` + `Enter`) phase-space seeding with `AccessKit` `Role::GraphicsDocument` metadata, `lock_canvas_arrow_keys`, `paint_canvas_focus_ring`, live `Max Lyapunov λ` and 3-body momentum readouts, 3-body perturbation control, and synchronized motion + energy-drift split views, capping ensembles at `16` orbits and `4,096` pre-allocated section points per orbit within `FRAME_BUDGET_MS` and `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4 KiB`).
   - Synchronize an explicit `RungeKutta4` ghost twin to plot live relative energy drift and export `CSV`, `JSON`, `PNG`, `SVG`, and `GIF` artifacts via `SimulationFramework`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `GaussLegendre4` bounds peak chaotic `DoublePendulum` relative energy error below `0.001%` (`< 1e-5` relative drift) across `10,000` steps at `dt = 0.02` (versus `>3.0%` secular energy loss under `RungeKutta4`), `ImplicitMidpoint` and `GaussLegendre4` preserve exact unit amplitude on undamped `HarmonicOscillator` runs in `OdeSolverTool`, and `Yoshida4` preserves `HenonHeilesSystem` KAM tori and `ThreeBodyChoreography` energy/momentum with zero heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
- **Positive**: Desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` users can interactively map KAM stability islands, monitor 4D/12D Lyapunov exponents, audit explicit vs. symplectic energy conservation, and round-trip presets, stateless URLs, and plot exports (`CSV`, `JSON`, `PNG`, `SVG`, `GIF`).
- **Neutral**: Barnes-Hut tree gravity, relativistic geodesics, and user-typed symbolic Hamiltonians remain out of scope so all state buffers stay compile-time stack-sized (`ArrayState<4>` and `ArrayState<12>`).
