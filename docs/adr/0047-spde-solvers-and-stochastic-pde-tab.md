# ADR 0047: Zero-Allocation Stochastic PDE Solvers, Statistical Mechanics Scaling Kernels, and StochasticPdeTab

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0008](0008-finite-state-guard-solver-divergence.md), [ADR 0021](0021-precompute-stencil-reciprocals.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
[`crates/domain_physics/README.md`](../../crates/domain_physics/README.md) and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/mod.rs) previously lacked non-equilibrium interface-growth and phase-field coarsening engines, leaving `stat_mech` with a placeholder `DUMMY = 1.0` verification entry and `traceability.toml` mapped to `"dummy"`. Meanwhile, [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/mod.rs) provided deterministic finite-difference `StencilOperators` and 1D/2D wave/reaction-diffusion solvers, but had no zero-allocation stochastic white-noise or conserved staggered-flux noise buffers, nor a pre-planned 2D spectral solver for fourth-order biharmonic operators ($\nabla^4$) whose explicit CFL limit $\Delta t \propto \Delta x^4$ makes interactive Cahn-Hilliard spinodal decomposition impractical.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Allocation SPDE Noise Buffers and Pre-Planned 2D Spectral Solver (`pure_math::analysis::pde::spde`)**:
   - Add [`SpatialNoiseBuffer`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/spde/noise.rs) backed by deterministic `OxidizeRng` draws for uncorrelated Gaussian space-time white noise (`fill_gaussian`) and discrete divergence-free/mass-conserving staggered-flux noise $\nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\xi}$ (`fill_conserved_flux_1d_periodic`, `fill_conserved_flux_1d_neumann`, `fill_conserved_flux_2d_periodic`) with machine-precision zero spatial mean ($\sum \eta_i = 0$).
   - Add [`SemiImplicitSpectral2D`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/spde/spectral_2d.rs) caching row/column `rustfft` plans, `Complex<f64>` transpose workspaces, and exact 5-point discrete negative-Laplacian symbols $q^2(k_x, k_y)$ to step stiff $-\nu \nabla^2$ and $-\kappa \nabla^4$ operators unconditionally stably with zero heap allocations per step, alongside circularly averaged radial structure-factor binning `compute_radial_structure_factor`.
2. **KPZ / Edwards-Wilkinson Interface Growth and Model A / B Phase-Field Coarsening (`domain_physics::physics::stat_mech`)**:
   - Add [`interface_growth`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/interface_growth/mod.rs) (`InterfaceGrowth1D`, `InterfaceGrowth2D`, `InterfaceScalingTelemetry`) using `StencilOperators`, Lam-Shin conservative $(\nabla h)^2$ discretization, and $O(\Delta t)$-consistent tamed Euler-Maruyama stepping, plus live roughness $W(L, t)$, Family-Vicsek data collapse, and OLS log-log growth-exponent $\beta$ regression.
   - Add [`phase_field`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/phase_field/mod.rs) (`PhaseFieldSolver2D`, `PhaseFieldTelemetry`) implementing non-conserved Allen-Cahn (Model A) and conserved Cahn-Hilliard (Model B) dynamics via Eyre convex-splitting + `SemiImplicitSpectral2D` with conserved thermal flux noise, discrete Ginzburg-Landau free energy $F_h[\phi]$, and radial structure-factor coarsening wavevectors $k_{\max}(t)$ and $k_1(t)$.
   - Replace the placeholder `stat_mech` `theory_verification!` block in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/mod.rs) with verified universal scaling and Model B conservation invariants, and bind `stat_mech`, `spde`, `interface_growth`, `phase_field`, and `stochastic_pde` in [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml).
3. **Auto-Discovered `StochasticPdeTab` (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Add [`StochasticPdeTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/mod.rs) (`// @explorer_feature = "physics"`, `url_slug = "stochastic_pde"`) exposing [`KpzInterfaceTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/kpz_tool.rs) and [`SpinodalDecompositionTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/stochastic_pde/spinodal_tool.rs), four `StarterPresetMetadata` cards, `FiniteStateGuard` rollback, `ToolStateBounds` + `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` validation, conserved zero-mean droplet brushing on Model B, `AccessKit` `Role::GraphicsDocument` canvases, WCAG 2.2 AA colormaps, and deferred PNG/SVG/CSV/JSON exports.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: All 1D/2D SPDE stepping and telemetry kernels execute with zero dynamic heap allocations under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, preserving $\beta_{\text{EW}} \approx 1/4$, $\beta_{\text{KPZ}} \approx 1/3$, strict deterministic Lyapunov free-energy decay $F_h[\phi^{n+1}] \le F_h[\phi^n]$, and Model B mass conservation $|\langle \phi(t) \rangle - \langle \phi(0) \rangle| < 10^{-12}$.
- **Positive**: `StochasticPdeTab` is discovered automatically by `math_explorer_gui/build.rs` without touching `tabs/mod.rs` or `app.rs`, keeping WASM bundle overhead minimal by reusing existing `rustfft` and `math_commons` dependencies.
- **Neutral**: 3D volumetric Cahn-Hilliard grids and coupled hydrodynamic Navier-Stokes (Model H) remain out of scope to preserve interactive 60 FPS desktop and `wasm32` frame budgets.
