# ADR 20261123: Developmental Phyllotaxis, Nutrient-Coupled L-Systems, and Multi-Tool Biology Playground

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0004](0004-unified-verification-dependency-audit.md), [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), [ADR 20261123](20261123-fft-lenia-engine-and-gray-scott-kinetics.md), [ADR 20261123](20261123-botanical-kernels-and-biology-studios.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
Following [ADR 20261123](20261123-fft-lenia-engine-and-gray-scott-kinetics.md) and [ADR 20261123](20261123-botanical-kernels-and-biology-studios.md), [`crates/domain_biology`](../../crates/domain_biology/README.md) implemented enzyme saturation (`MichaelisMenten`, `HillKinetics`), evolutionary replicator games (`HawkDovePopulation`), generic $N$-species reaction-diffusion (`ReactionDiffusionSystem`), two-species Schnakenberg and Gray-Scott morphogenesis, spectral 2D `LeniaEngine`, Vogel spiral `VogelPhyllotaxisEngine`, and bounded `LSystemEngine`, while [`MorphogenesisTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/mod.rs) hosted `MorphogenesisTool`, `LeniaTool`, and `PhyllotaxisLSystemTool`. However, plastochron state stepping (`PhyllotaxisState`), direct biochemical modulation (`NutrientCoupling`) of `LSystemEngine` turtle internode and branching parameters, and a unified replicator-and-kinetics exploration studio inside `MorphogenesisTab` remained to be integrated alongside a dedicated morphogenesis & Lenia manuscript.

## Decision
1. **Developmental Phyllotaxis State and Nutrient-Coupled L-Systems (`biology::phyllotaxis::nutrient`)**:
   - Implement [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/phyllotaxis/nutrient.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/phyllotaxis/nutrient.rs) re-exported from [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/phyllotaxis/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/phyllotaxis/mod.rs), providing `PhyllotaxisConfig`, `PhyllotaxisState` (delegating to `VogelPhyllotaxisEngine`), and `NutrientCoupling` (`MichaelisMenten` and `HillKinetics` scaling of `TurtleConfig`) so `PhyllotaxisState::step` and `LSystemEngine::derive_bounded` execute with zero heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`.
2. **Multi-Tool `MorphogenesisTab` Expansion and Starter Presets**:
   - Extend [`PhyllotaxisLSystemTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/phyllotaxis_lsystem/mod.rs) with optional nutrient-kinetics shoot modulation and register [`EvolutionKineticsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/evolution_kinetics_tool.rs) alongside [`MorphogenesisTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/tool.rs) and [`LeniaTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/lenia/mod.rs) under `domain: "biology"` with `StarterPresetMetadata` cards (`gray_scott_coral`, `phyllotaxis_hill_shoot`, `evolution_hawk_dove_ess`), `FiniteStateGuard` recovery, `AccessKit` keyboard-focusable canvas roles, and lossless URL fragment state round-trips.
3. **Academic Traceability and API Baseline**:
   - Author [`papers/morphogenesis_and_lenia.tex`](../../papers/morphogenesis_and_lenia.tex) and map `morphogenesis_and_lenia = "morphogenesis_and_lenia.tex"` alongside `computational_biology.tex` in [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml).
   - Regenerate [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json) via `unified_verification regenerate-baseline`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `MorphogenesisTab` exposes a complete four-tool mathematical biology suite (`MorphogenesisTool`, `LeniaTool`, `PhyllotaxisLSystemTool`, and `EvolutionKineticsTool`) without duplicating `LeniaEngine` or `GrayScottKinetics` from [ADR 20261123](20261123-fft-lenia-engine-and-gray-scott-kinetics.md) and with zero heap allocations inside verified step loops.
- **Positive**: Every `domain_biology` submodule (`neuroscience`, `kinetics`, `reaction_diffusion`, `morphogenesis`, `lenia`, `phyllotaxis`, `evolution`, `diffusion`) is exercised by interactive GUI visualizations, documented in [`crates/domain_biology/README.md`](../../crates/domain_biology/README.md), and linked to [`papers/morphogenesis_and_lenia.tex`](../../papers/morphogenesis_and_lenia.tex).
- **Neutral**: Public additions in `domain_biology` require regenerating [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json).
