# ADR 0047: Hybrid Analytical and Zero-Allocation Monte Carlo Edge Validation and Bankroll Solvency

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), [ADR 0035](0035-kelly-net-odds-seeded-rng-and-paradox-labs.md), and [ADR 0042](0042-hierarchical-umbrella-errors-and-diagnostic-bus-helpers.md))

## Context
Previously, `pure_math::statistics::kelly` computed optimal Kelly wagering fractions (`kelly_fraction`, `fractional_kelly`, `expected_growth_rate`) and stated in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/kelly/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/kelly/mod.rs) that for $f \le f^*$ the probability of ruin is zero. While true for continuous zero-wealth bankruptcy ($\lim_{t \to \infty} P(W_t = 0) = 0$) under infinitely divisible stakes, real bankrolls face finite drawdown barriers (e.g., a 50% loss floor or table minimum stake) where Full Kelly incurs an exact **50%** asymptotic drawdown probability and ~38% finite-horizon hit rate over 200 even-money bets at $p = 0.55$. In addition, OxidizeMath lacked post-bet pricing validation (Closing Line Value, Brier/log-loss calibration against devigged closing lines, and heterogeneous-odds Bernoulli yield significance) and relied on a GUI-only 64-path simulator in `BankrollEnsembleLab`.

## Decision
1. **Post-Bet Edge Validation (`pure_math::statistics::validation`)**:
   - Introduce [`clv`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/validation/clv.rs) implementing single-bet CLV (`ev_fraction`, `ev_pct`, `implied_prob_edge`, `log_odds_shift` per Kaunitz et al., 2017), two-way and multi-way closing-line vigorish removal (`devig_two_way`, `devig_multi_way_into`, `devig_multi_way`), and single-pass zero-allocation Welford ledger summaries (`summarize_clv`).
   - Introduce [`calibration`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/validation/calibration.rs) implementing `brier_score`, strict open-interval $(0, 1)$ `binary_log_loss` (`ln_1p(-p)`), and one-pass `compare_calibration` against devigged closing lines.
   - Introduce [`significance`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/validation/significance.rs) computing exact heterogeneous-odds Bernoulli profit variance, realized yield z-scores, one-sided and two-sided p-values, and required 95%-significance sample sizes (`required_bets_for_95_significance`).
2. **Hybrid Analytical & Monte Carlo Bankroll Solvency (`pure_math::statistics::ruin`)**:
   - Introduce [`analytical`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/ruin/analytical.rs) implementing exact two-barrier and infinite-horizon Gambler's Ruin (`gamblers_ruin_with_target`, `gamblers_ruin_infinite`) with fair-game ($p = 0.5$) limits and overflow-free $p < 0.5$ scaling, plus heterogeneous portfolio Cramér-Lundberg adjustment coefficient solving ($E[\exp(-r X)] = 1$) via `analysis::roots::Bisection` bracketing and safeguarded `NewtonRaphson` polishing (`solve_lundberg_ruin`).
   - Introduce [`barrier`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/ruin/barrier.rs) computing proportional-Kelly drawdown barrier probabilities via $(B_{\text{floor}} / W_0)^{2 \mu / \sigma^2}$ Brownian diffusion approximations and exact log-return Lundberg exponents solving $E[(1 + f R)^{-\theta}] = 1$ (`kelly_barrier_risk`, `portfolio_kelly_barrier_risk`), and clarify continuous-zero vs. finite-barrier ruin in `statistics/kelly/mod.rs`.
   - Introduce [`ensemble`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/ruin/ensemble.rs) providing `BankrollEnsembleWorkspace`, a preallocated `OxidizeRng` workspace modeled on `ParrondoEnsemble` that simulates 10,000+ paths with zero heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, reporting finite-horizon barrier ruin, 95% Wilson intervals (`wilson_interval_95`), and pathwise maximum-drawdown quantiles.
3. **Zero-Allocation `KellyError` Extensions & GUI Workbench Upgrades**:
   - Extend `KellyError` (`Copy`, zero heap fields) with `InvalidStake`, `InvalidBarrier`, `InsufficientBets`, `DimensionMismatch`, and `ConvergenceFailed`, re-exported through `PureMathError` and `math_explorer`.
   - Upgrade [`BankrollEnsembleLab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/financial_math/bankroll_ensemble.rs), [`BankrollGrowthTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/financial_math/bankroll_growth.rs), and [`BetSizeCalculatorTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/financial_math/bet_size.rs) to consume `BankrollEnsembleWorkspace`, `kelly_barrier_risk`, `solve_single_bet_lundberg`, `required_bets_for_95_significance`, and `single_bet_clv` while preserving 100% backward-compatible JSON preset serialization.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Unifies pre-settlement pricing validation (CLV and proper scoring rules) with analytical and finite-horizon bankroll solvency across `pure_math`, `math_explorer`, and `math_explorer_gui` under `VerifiedAllocator` zero-allocation locks and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` compatibility.
- **Neutral**: Adds `statistics::validation`, `statistics::ruin`, and five `Copy` `KellyError` variants to `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json` while keeping GUI tab, tool, and starter-preset counts unchanged.
