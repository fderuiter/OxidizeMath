# ADR 20261123: Cut-and-Project Quasicrystals, Zero-Allocation Bragg Diffraction, Quasiperiodic Bands, and Interactive BCS/Phonon/Screening Tools

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0013](0013-retire-legacy-bcs-gap-solver.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), [ADR 0032](0032-high-contrast-focus-selection-and-raster-tokens.md), and [ADR 0044](0044-unify-build-time-traceability-engine.md))

## Context
[`crates/domain_physics/README.md`](../../crates/domain_physics/README.md) advertised reciprocal-space calculations alongside crystal lattices and electronic band structures, while [`math_explorer/README.md`](../../math_explorer/README.md) summarized `solid_state` solely as `(BCS Superconductivity, Ising)`. In practice, [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/mod.rs) lacked reciprocal-lattice, Brillouin-zone, kinematic Bragg diffraction, cut-and-project quasicrystal, and quasiperiodic tight-binding kernels; `solid_state/mod.rs` used a placeholder `DUMMY = 1.0` invariant; `traceability.toml` and `SolidStateTab` misattributed solid-state code to `mmwave_radiotherapy_setup.tex`, `modular_polynomials_review`, and `clinical_trials_statistics`; and existing `bcs`, `phonons`, `interactions`, and `screening` kernels had no interactive GUI counterparts in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/mod.rs).

## Decision
1. **Cut-and-Project Quasicrystals & Phason Dynamics (`domain_physics::physics::solid_state::quasicrystal`)**:
   - Implement preallocated [`QuasicrystalWorkspace`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/quasicrystal.rs) and [`generate_quasicrystal_patch`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/quasicrystal.rs) supporting 1D Fibonacci ($\mathbb{Z}^2$), 2D 5-fold and 10-fold Penrose ($\mathbb{Z}^5$), 2D 8-fold Ammann-Beenker ($\mathbb{Z}^4$), and 2D 12-fold Stampfli ($\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_{12}] \cong \mathbb{Z}^4$) projections with continuous perpendicular-space phason shifts $\boldsymbol{\phi}_\perp$ and discrete vertex flip detection.
2. **Reciprocal Space, Brillouin Zones, & Zero-Allocation Bragg Solver (`domain_physics::physics::solid_state::reciprocal`)**:
   - Add [`reciprocal_basis_3d`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), [`reciprocal_basis_2d`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), [`brillouin_zone_1d`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), [`first_brillouin_zone_2d`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), [`pseudo_brillouin_zone_polygon`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), [`unit_cell_structure_factor`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs), and separable phasor-recurrence [`BraggWorkspace`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/reciprocal.rs) evaluating $S(\mathbf{q}) = \frac{1}{N}\left|\sum_j e^{-i\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{r}_j}\right|^2$ with zero heap allocations under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
3. **1D Quasiperiodic Tight-Binding Eigensolver & Zero-Alloc BCS Window (`domain_physics::physics::solid_state::{quasiperiodic,bcs}`)**:
   - Add [`QuasiperiodicWorkspace`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/quasiperiodic.rs) solving finite Fibonacci and Aubry-André symmetric tridiagonal Hamiltonians via implicit Wilkinson-shifted QL iteration in preallocated buffers, exposing sorted eigenenergies, devil's-staircase IDOS, and eigenstate IPRs.
   - Extend [`IsotropicBCSModel`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs) with `with_uniform_window` and zero-allocation `update_uniform_window`, and replace `DUMMY = 1.0` in `solid_state/mod.rs` with physical golden-ratio, reciprocal-orthogonality, BCC-reflection, and Bogoliubov-unitarity invariants.
4. **Interactive `SolidStateTab` Expansion & Traceability Reconciliation (`math_explorer_gui`, `traceability.toml`, `papers/references.bib`)**:
   - Add [`QuasicrystalDiffractionTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/quasicrystal_diffraction.rs) (dual real-space tiling/phason-flip plot + `AccessKit`-focusable Bragg diffraction canvas using `heatmap_color` / `heatmap_color_high_contrast`), [`BcsSuperconductivityTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/bcs_superconductivity.rs) (`BcsGapSolver`, `coherence_factors_strong`, `phonons`, `FrohlichVertex`, and `screening`), reciprocal/extinction readouts in [`CrystalViewer`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/crystal_viewer.rs), and backwards-compatible Fibonacci/Aubry-André regimes in [`BandStructureTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/band_structure.rs), backed by 6 new `StarterPresetMetadata` cards and canonical BibTeX DOIs.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Steady-state 2D Bragg $S(\mathbf{q})$ and `BcsGapSolver` sweeps allocate `0` heap bytes under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, HighContrast Bragg rasters guarantee WCAG 2.2 `>= 3.0:1` contrast against `#000000`, and legacy periodic `BandStructureTool` presets round-trip byte-identically alongside new quasiperiodic and BCS URL states.
- **Neutral**: Standalone static CAD/DXF/SVG tiling fabrication exporters remain scoped to their dedicated geometry/fabrication workflows rather than duplicating mesh/CAD exporters inside `SolidStateTab`.
