# ADR 20261123: Root-Owned Pane Dataflow Bus and Cycle-Checked Cross-Domain Pipelines

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0009](0009-reusable-graph-layout-algorithms.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), [ADR 0039](0039-metric-agnostic-3d-tda-exact-z2-homology-and-chaos-mashup.md), and [ADR 0039](0039-network-epidemic-containment-primitives.md))

## Context
Multi-pane viewport layouts (`SplitHorizontal`, `SplitVertical`, and `QuadGrid`) previously isolated simulation tools into independent domain silos, with a single exception: `vietoris_rips/handoff.rs` used process-global `OnceLock<Mutex<...>>` statics to pass 3D Lorenz landmarks to `VietorisRipsTool`. Process-global statics cannot distinguish between multiple panes or concurrent `egui::Context` instances in parallel unit tests, risk holding locks across UI callbacks, and cannot persist cross-pane couplings in `.mws` workspace envelopes or shareable URLs. Supporting live cross-domain experiments—such as editing a custom network in `GraphEditorTool` while observing heterogeneous epidemic thresholds $R_0^{\text{eff}}$ in `NetworkPropagationTool` and Kuramoto phase synchronization $r(t)$ in `NeuralNetworkVizTool`, or streaming `AttractorPlotter` landmarks and `MorphogenesisTool` reaction-diffusion fields into `VietorisRipsTool` and `SurfaceViewer`—imposes four architectural constraints:
1. **Zero Locks Held Across `ExplorerTab::show_inside`**: Tool render callbacks may trigger nested UI interactions, command palette dispatches, and undo snapshots; dataflow routing must stage immutable `Arc` payloads around `show_inside` without holding any `Mutex` or `RwLock`.
2. **Acyclic Topology & Slot Lifecycle Invariants**: Directed cross-pane links must reject self-loops and multi-hop cycles (`A -> ... -> A`), remap endpoint indices deterministically across `swap_panes`, `rotate_panes`, and `clone_pane_to`, and prune out-of-range links when collapsing to fewer panes.
3. **Zero-Allocation Solver Hot Loops**: Downstream epidemic (`NetworkEpidemicModel::step`), biophysical (`HodgkinHuxleyNeuron::step`), and phase-oscillator (`KuramotoNetwork::step`) step loops must execute with `0` heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`, resizing buffers only when an upstream topology revision advances.
4. **Bounded `.mws` & Share URL Persistence**: Multi-pane pipeline topologies and per-pane tool states must round-trip atomically through `.mws` `WorkspaceEnvelope` files and `#...&pipe=<base64url>` share links within `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4,096` bytes) and a `200`-vertex cap.

## Decision
1. **Root-Owned `PaneDataflowBus` & `PanePipelineDag`**:
   - Introduce [`math_explorer_gui/src/app/dataflow_bus.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/dataflow_bus.rs) and [`math_explorer_gui/src/app/dataflow_bus_staging.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/dataflow_bus_staging.rs) owned directly on `MathExplorerApp` (`dataflow_bus` and `pipeline_dag`).
   - Support three sanitized stream payloads: `GraphTopologyPayload` (`<= 200` nodes, `<= 2,000` edges), `LandmarkCloud3DPayload` (`<= 64` finite 3D points), and `ScalarField2DPayload` (`<= 64x64` downsampled grid).
   - Wrap `tab.show_inside(ui)` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/app/viewport/pane_chrome.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/viewport/pane_chrome.rs) with `stage_pane_inputs` and `commit_pane_outputs`, exposing `⚡ Pipe` source menus in pane headers with polite/assertive `TwinSlotLiveRegion` ARIA announcements.
   - Retire all `OnceLock<Mutex<...>>` globals from [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/vietoris_rips/handoff.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/vietoris_rips/handoff.rs) in favor of `egui::Context`-scoped one-click handoffs and `PaneDataflowBus` live streams.
2. **Zero-Allocation `KuramotoNetwork` & Cross-Domain Tool Adapters**:
   - Add `#[verified_engine::verified]` `KuramotoNetwork` in [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/neuroscience/kuramoto.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/neuroscience/kuramoto.rs) integrating coupled RK4 phase dynamics $\dot{\theta}_i = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_j A_{ji}\sin(\theta_j - \theta_i)$ and order parameter $r(t)e^{i\psi(t)} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_j e^{i\theta_j(t)}$ with `0` heap allocations per step.
   - Couple `GraphEditorTool` ([`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/graph_editor/dataflow.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/graph_editor/dataflow.rs)) to `NetworkPropagationTool` via `NetworkEpidemicModel::builder()` ([`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/epidemiology/network_propagation/dataflow.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/epidemiology/network_propagation/dataflow.rs)) and to `NeuralNetworkVizTool` (`HodgkinHuxley` + `Kuramoto` modes in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/neuroscience/kuramoto_ui.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/neuroscience/kuramoto_ui.rs)).
   - Stream `AttractorPlotter` 3D landmarks and `MorphogenesisTool` 2D reaction-diffusion fields into `VietorisRipsTool` and `SurfaceViewer` (`DiscreteFieldHeightSurface` and `LandmarkShepardSurface` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/surface_viewer/field_surface.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/surface_viewer/field_surface.rs)).
3. **`.mws` Envelope & `&pipe=` Share URL Codec**:
   - Persist `pipeline_links: Vec<PaneLink>` on `AppState` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/app/state.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/state.rs) and encode multi-pane pipelines via [`math_explorer_gui/src/url_state/pipeline.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/url_state/pipeline.rs), enforcing atomic DAG cycle validation, `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4 KiB`), and `MAX_DATAFLOW_GRAPH_NODES` (`200`) on decode while keeping single-pane URLs unchanged.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can wire live acyclic pipelines across Graph Theory, Epidemiology, Neuroscience, Chaos, Morphogenesis, Differential Geometry, and TDA panes with zero lock contention across `show_inside`, `0`-allocation solver steps, full screen-reader feedback, and `.mws` / URL reproducibility.
- **Neutral**: Single-pane and unlinked multi-pane workspaces incur zero additional URL parameters or solver overhead.
