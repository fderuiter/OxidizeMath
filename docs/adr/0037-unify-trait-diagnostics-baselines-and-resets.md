# ADR 0037: Unify Trait Diagnostics, Dual Baselines, and Simulation Resets

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0008](0008-finite-state-guard-solver-divergence.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), and [ADR 0036](0036-delete-simulation-model-traits-and-physics-adapters.md))

## Context
1. **Fragmented Divergence Banners & Wall-Clock Diagnostics**: `SimulationFramework::check_finite_state_guard` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/state_guard.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/state_guard.rs) previously lacked a structured `InteractiveTool` diagnostic hook, synthesized fallback reports using `self.start_time.elapsed().as_secs_f64()` (reporting session uptime rather than solver time $t$), and short-circuited once `self.is_diverged` was set. Meanwhile, individual tools (`OdeSolverTool`, `CeraTool`, `UnifiedSimTool`, `WaveSimulatorTool`, `AttractorPlotter`, `MriTool`) rendered duplicate local warning banners inside sidebars or central viewports.
2. **Single-Slot Baseline Overwrite**: Loading a Starter Gallery card, JSON preset file, or URL share envelope overwrote `SimulationFramework::baseline_tool_state`, erasing the tool's initial factory defaults until the application restarted. `BankrollGrowthTool` also rendered a conflicting sidebar `↻ Reset to Defaults` button alongside the shared framework toolbar.
3. **Destructive Playback Resets & Stale Preset Loads**: Playback reset (`reset()`) in `MriTool` and `NeuralNetworkVizTool` wiped user-configured relaxation sliders or custom synaptic weights, whereas `try_load_state()` in `MriTool` and `IsingTool` left stale Bloch trajectory histories or spin lattices and unrefreshed thermodynamic observables (`energy`, `magnetization`) until manual stepping. `AttractorPlotter::is_paused` omitted `SolverExecutionState::Ready`, causing reset and freshly loaded attractors to integrate immediately instead of holding at initial coordinates `(1, 1, 1)`.

## Decision
1. **Structured Solver Diagnostics & Single Shared Recovery Banner**:
   - Add `InteractiveTool::diagnostic_report(&self) -> Option<DiagnosticReport>` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/mod.rs) and `SimulationRunner::diagnostic_report(&self) -> Option<DiagnosticReport>` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/mod.rs), surfacing solver `t_diverged`, `step_index`, `variable`, and `message` from `OdeSolverTool`, `CeraTool`, `UnifiedSimTool`, `WaveSimulatorTool`, `AttractorPlotter`, and `MriTool` without wall-clock uptime.
   - Recheck active tool finiteness every frame in `SimulationFramework::check_finite_state_guard` (under 1 ms without calling `save_state()`), auto-dismissing `is_diverged` and `diagnostic_report` as soon as playback reset, valid parameter edits, or banner remedies restore finite state, and removing all duplicate local divergence banners across the 7 diverging `SimulationFramework` tools.
2. **Dual Factory and Active-Preset Baselines**:
   - Track `factory_tool_state`, `preset_tool_state`, and per-tool `preset_tool_states` in `SimulationFramework` (persisted backward-compatibly via `#[serde(default)]` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/persistence.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/persistence.rs) and synchronized in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/state_guard/baselines.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/state_guard/baselines.rs)).
   - Render separate `↺ Revert to Preset` and `↻ Factory Defaults` actions in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_preset_bar.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_preset_bar.rs) whenever an active preset is loaded (`↻ Reset Parameters` otherwise), and remove the duplicate `↻ Reset to Defaults` button from [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/financial_math/bankroll_growth.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/financial_math/bankroll_growth.rs).
3. **Non-Destructive Playback Reset vs Immediate Preset State Refresh**:
   - Keep `MriTool::reset()` and `NeuralNetworkVizTool::reset()` non-destructive to user sliders and custom synaptic weights while restarting magnetization/voltage state, refresh Bloch histories (`MriTool::try_load_state`) and spin lattices/observables (`IsingTool::try_load_state`) immediately on state load, and treat `SolverExecutionState::Ready` as paused in `AttractorPlotter::is_paused(&self)` so reset holds at `(1, 1, 1)`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Every diverging `SimulationFramework` tool surfaces solver-accurate `t`, `Step`, and `Variable` diagnostics through one WCAG 2.2 AA top recovery banner that auto-dismisses upon valid recovery, preserves both preset and factory baselines across sessions, and enforces consistent playback-reset vs preset-load semantics.
- **Neutral**: Standalone tabs outside `SimulationFramework` and `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json` remain unchanged.
