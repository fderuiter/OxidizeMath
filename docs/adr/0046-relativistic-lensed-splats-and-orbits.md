# ADR 0046: Zero-Allocation Fixed-Size Christoffel Symbols, Relativistic Thin-Lens Splat Jacobians, and Paczyński-Wiita Galactic Orbits

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0040](0040-galactic-dynamics-and-astrophysics-tab.md) and [ADR 0044](0044-geodesic-engine-and-relativity-tab.md))

## Context
1. **Heap-Allocated Metric Connection Evaluation**: [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs) previously evaluated $\Gamma^k_{ij}$ exclusively via heap-allocated `DMatrix<f64>` and `Vec` buffers (`christoffel_symbols`), preventing `VerifiedAllocator`-verified zero-allocation connection checks inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
2. **Uncoupled EWA Gaussian Splatting and Relativistic Astrophysics**: [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/gaussian_splatting/projection.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/gaussian_splatting/projection.rs) projected 3D Gaussians through unlensed pinhole Jacobians only, while [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/mod.rs) lacked closed-form gravitational thin-lens deflections, $2 \times 2$ magnification/shear Jacobians for `SchwarzschildBlackHole`, `NfwCusp`, and `BurkertCore`, and central Paczyński-Wiita black-hole pseudo-potentials coupled to `gravitational_time_dilation`, `calculate_lorentz_factor`, and `synchrotron_power`.
3. **Crate-DAG Separation Requirement**: `domain_ai` must remain strictly decoupled from `domain_physics` so neural rendering primitives can compose arbitrary $2 \times 2$ spatial lens Jacobians without domain inversion.

## Decision
1. **Stack-Allocated `christoffel_symbols_fixed<const D: usize>` (`pure_math::tensor`)**:
   - Add `christoffel_symbols_fixed` in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs) operating exclusively on `nalgebra::SMatrix<f64, D, D>` and `[f64; D]` stack buffers with analytical LU inversion (`try_inverse`) and central finite differences, achieving zero heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`.
2. **Closed-Form Thin-Lens Deflections & $2 \times 2$ Shear Jacobians (`domain_physics::physics::astrophysics`)**:
   - Add [`lensing.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/lensing.rs) and [`halo_lensing.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/halo_lensing.rs) computing dual-image Schwarzschild Einstein-ring maps $\theta_\pm = \frac{1}{2}(\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 + 4\theta_E^2})$, photon-sphere ($1.5 R_s$) shadow masking via `SchwarzschildBlackHole::photon_sphere_radius`, Einstein-ring singularity clamping ($|\theta - \theta_E| \ge 10^{-3}\theta_E$), and closed-form Bartelmann (1996) `NfwCusp`, `BurkertCore`, and Kuzmin-disk convergence $\kappa$, mean convergence $\bar{\kappa}$, tangential shear $\gamma$, and magnification Jacobians $\mathbf{M} = (\partial\boldsymbol{\beta}/\partial\boldsymbol{\theta})^{-1}$.
3. **Domain-Agnostic Lensed Splat Projection (`domain_ai::ai::gaussian_splatting`)**:
   - Add `LensedSplatImageSpec`, `LensedProjectionOutcome`, `project_lensed_covariance`, and `project_scene_lensed_into` in [`projection.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/gaussian_splatting/projection.rs) accepting generic `Matrix2<f64>` lens Jacobians and scalar radiance weights to emit up to two `Gaussian2D` images per `Gaussian3D` ($\Sigma_{2\text{D,lensed}} = \mathbf{M}\,\Sigma_{2\text{D}}\,\mathbf{M}^\top$) into pre-allocated `2 * MAX_DYNAMIC_SPLATS` buffers with zero heap allocations and zero dependency on `domain_physics`.
4. **Paczyński-Wiita Relativistic Orbits & Beaming (`paczynski_wiita.rs` & `orbits.rs`)**:
   - Add [`paczynski_wiita.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/paczynski_wiita.rs) wiring $\Phi_{\text{PW}}(r) = -GM_{\text{BH}} / (r - R_s)$ into `GalacticDiskSystem` with clean particle capture at `SchwarzschildBlackHole::isco` ($3 R_s$), weak-field radial acceleration validation against `christoffel_symbols_fixed`, and clamped relativistic Doppler/synchrotron beaming ($r > 1.001 R_s$, $v \le 0.998\,999\,c$).
5. **Interactive GUI Presets, Caustic Overlays, URL Persistence, and Exports (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Add [`volumetric_accretion_preset.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/volumetric_accretion_preset.rs) (`VolumetricSplatPreset::RelativisticAccretionDisk`) and [`volumetric_scene_lensing.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/volumetric_scene_lensing.rs) (`72x72`, `MAX_DYNAMIC_SPLATS = 384`, `768` dual `Gaussian2D` slots), interactive canvas lens dragging, `NfwCusp` vs. `BurkertCore` caustic overlays, backwards-compatible `#[serde(default)]` URL state sharing, and PNG, SVG, PLY, and CSV exports across `AiTab` and `AstrophysicsTab`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Hot-path 4D Christoffel evaluation, $72 \times 72$ dual-image lensed EWA splat projection/rasterization, and symplectic Paczyński-Wiita orbit bursts run with zero heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`.
- **Positive**: `domain_ai` remains free of `domain_physics` dependencies while `math_explorer_gui` couples relativistic time dilation, Lorentz factors, synchrotron power, photon-sphere shadows, and dark-matter halo caustics interactively on native desktop and `wasm32`.
- **Neutral**: Existing unlensed `GaussianSplattingTool`, `RotationCurveExplorer`, and `GalacticOrbitSimulator` preset JSONs and `#tab=` URLs deserialize identically via `#[serde(default)]`.
