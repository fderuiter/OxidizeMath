# ADR 20261123: Zero-Allocation Curvilinear Vector Calculus, Flat-Buffer Geometric Flows, and Interactive 3D Vector, Flow, and Riemann Studios

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0003](0003-traceability-refactoring.md) and [ADR 0041](0041-volumetric-field-studio-zero-alloc-3d-kernels.md))

## Context
[`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/vector_calculus/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/vector_calculus/mod.rs), [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/mean_curvature_flow.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/mean_curvature_flow.rs), [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/heat_equation.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/heat_equation.rs), and [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/complex/mod.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/complex/mod.rs) previously lacked interactive 3D GUI counterparts in `math_explorer_gui`. In addition, `DiscreteSurface`, `MeanCurvatureFlow`, and `HeatEquationSolver` stored nested `Vec<Vec<...>>` grids and allocated temporary heap vectors inside `step()`, violating NASA Power of 10 Rule 3 (`VerifiedAllocator` zero-allocation hot-loop requirement), while `vector_calculus` cited an unrelated number-theory paper (`quasiperfect_alcf.tex`) and lacked Kelvin-Stokes surface-vs-loop verification and 3D Helmholtz-Hodge decomposition.

## Decision
1. **Traceability Repair & Zero-Allocation Curvilinear Vector Calculus (`pure_math::vector_calculus`)**:
   - Map `vector_calculus = "tensors.tex"` in [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml), update citations across `vector_calculus` to `// [cite:vector_calculus]`, and bind `quasiperfect_alcf` to [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/number_theory/alcf.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/number_theory/alcf.rs) so zero `.tex` sources are orphaned.
   - Add coordinate-axis singularity guards to [`operators.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/vector_calculus/operators.rs), add `#[verified_engine::verified]` `StokesPatch` and `verify_stokes_theorem` to [`theorems.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/vector_calculus/theorems.rs), and implement pre-allocated `HelmholtzWorkspace` and zero-allocation `helmholtz_decompose` in [`helmholtz.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/vector_calculus/helmholtz.rs) using `math_commons::math_kernel::types::flatten_3d_index`.
2. **Contiguous Flat-Buffer Geometric Flows (`pure_math::differential_geometry`)**:
   - Refactor `DiscreteSurface`, `MeanCurvatureFlow`, and `HeatEquationSolver` onto contiguous 1D `Vec<Point3<f64>>` and `Vec<f64>` buffers indexed via `math_commons::math_kernel::types::flatten_2d_index`, pre-allocating double-buffered scratch storage in `new()` so `step()` performs zero dynamic heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`, and exposing explicit parabolic `recommended_cfl_dt`, `surface_area`, and `total_heat` diagnostics.
3. **Three Interactive 3D Studios on `Plot3DViewport` (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Ship [`CurvilinearVectorStudio`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/curvilinear_vector.rs) (`AnalysisTab`) for Cartesian, cylindrical, and spherical differential operators, Gauss/Stokes integral residuals, and 3D Helmholtz quiver splitting (`<= 32^3` cells).
   - Ship [`ManifoldFlowStudio`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/manifold_flow.rs) (`GeometryTopologyTab`) reusing `CustomSurfaceSandbox` and `PlaybackTransport`, bounding 2D surface grids by `MAX_INTERACTIVE_SURFACE_RESOLUTION`, clamping explicit steps to `recommended_cfl_dt`, guarding against numerical blow-up via `FiniteStateGuard`, and exporting watertight outward-oriented `.stl`, vertex-colored `.ply`, and `.obj` solids.
   - Ship [`RiemannSurfaceStudio`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/riemann_surface.rs) (`AnalysisTab`) combining multi-sheet 3D `NthRootSurface` and `LogSurface` monodromy path lifting with synchronized 2D `SchwarzChristoffel` polygon mapping, `schwarz_reflect` analytic continuation, and Cauchy integral/derivative/residue verification while keeping existing `SurfaceViewer` and `ComplexMappingTool` presets intact.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `MeanCurvatureFlow::step`, `HeatEquationSolver::step`, and `helmholtz_decompose` execute with `0` heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, and `100%` of public symbols across `vector_calculus`, `differential_geometry` flows, and `analysis::complex` are exercised interactively on desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` with `< 4 KiB` stateless URL presets.
- **Neutral**: Expands the `math_explorer_gui` catalog to `94` registered tools (`74` plot+dataset tools, `9` solid 3D tools) and `166` starter presets while leaving unstructured tetrahedral remeshing out of scope.
