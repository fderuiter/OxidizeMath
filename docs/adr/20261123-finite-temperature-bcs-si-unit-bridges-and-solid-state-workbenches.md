# ADR 20261123: Finite-Temperature BCS Superconductivity, CODATA SI Unit Bridges, and Solid-State Workbenches

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0013](0013-retire-legacy-bcs-gap-solver.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0037](0037-retire-physics-compatibility-shims.md))

## Context
[`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs) previously evaluated [`IsotropicBCSModel`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs) strictly at $T = 0\text{ K}$ without thermal $\tanh(E_k / (2 k_B T))$ weighting, pinned zero-seed initial guesses (`ElectronVolts::new(0.0)`) to the trivial $\Delta = 0$ fixed point even below $T_c$, and lacked smooth normal-state Bogoliubov coherence limits or Dynes-broadened NIS tunneling spectroscopy. Simultaneously, [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/quantum_stats.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/quantum_stats.rs) and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/ensembles.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/ensembles.rs) divided raw `f64` inputs directly by $k_B = 1.380\,649 \times 10^{-23}\text{ J/K}$ without typed [`ElectronVolts`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/types.rs) / [`Kelvin`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/types.rs) SI Joule bridges, causing $0.1\text{ eV}$ inputs at $300\text{ K}$ to evaluate $\exp(-0.1 / (1.38 \times 10^{-23} \cdot 300)) = 0$ or clamp Fermi-Dirac occupancy to an unphysical `0.49` when raw `eV` numbers were mixed with SI Joules. Finally, [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/mod.rs) exposed only `BandStructureTool`, `CrystalViewerTool`, and `IsingModelTool`, leaving BCS superconductivity, Bogoliubov quasiparticles, Fröhlich electron-phonon vertices, Debye phonons, Thomas-Fermi/Yukawa screening, Heisenberg magnons, second quantization, quantum occupancy, and canonical ensembles without interactive GUI workbenches.

## Decision
1. **Exact CODATA 2018 SI Unit Bridges & Stabilized Ensembles (`domain_physics`)**:
   - Extend [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/types.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/types.rs) with `ELEMENTARY_CHARGE_C = 1.602_176_634e-19`, `ElectronVolts::{to_joules, from_joules}`, and `Kelvin::{to_joules, from_joules, thermal_energy_ev, from_thermal_energy_ev}` backed by `KB`.
   - Add `calculate_occupancy_ev` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/quantum_stats.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/quantum_stats.rs) and log-sum-exp ground-state-shifted `CanonicalThermodynamics` helpers (`calculate_partition_function_ev`, `helmholtz_free_energy_ev`, `average_energy_ev`, `entropy_ev_per_kelvin`, `canonical_thermodynamics_ev`) in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/ensembles.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/ensembles.rs), preserving existing raw-Joule signatures per [ADR 0037](0037-retire-physics-compatibility-shims.md).
2. **Finite-Temperature BCS Gap Kernel, Normal-State Coherence Limits, and Dynes NIS Conductance (`domain_physics`)**:
   - Extend `IsotropicBCSModel` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs.rs) with `temperature: Kelvin` (defaulting to `0.0 K` in `IsotropicBCSModel::new` per [ADR 0013](0013-retire-legacy-bcs-gap-solver.md)), `with_temperature`, `pairing_kernel`, `critical_temperature`, finite-temperature $\tanh(E_k / (2 k_B T)) / (2 E_k)$ weighting, non-trivial condensation probing when `current_gap == 0.0` for $T < T_c$, and exact $\Delta(T) = 0.0\text{ eV}$ closure for $T \ge T_c$.
   - Add `quasiparticle_energy` and `coherence_factors_normal_limit` beside `coherence_factors_strong` (preserving `coherence_factors_strong(0, 0).is_err()`), plus zero-allocation 32-point Gauss-Legendre thermally convolved `nis_tunneling_conductance` and `dynes_dos_ratio` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs_tunneling.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/solid_state/bcs_tunneling.rs).
3. **Local-First `BcsSuperconductivityTool`, `QuantumStatsPhononTool`, and Solid-State Datasheets (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Register [`BcsSuperconductivityTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/bcs/mod.rs) and [`QuantumStatsPhononTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/quantum_stats/mod.rs) in [`SolidStateTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/mod.rs) alongside upgraded [`BandStructureTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/band_structure.rs), adding four `StarterPresetMetadata` cards (`bcs_niobium_nis_starter`, `bcs_normal_transition_starter`, `quantum_stats_fermi_bose_starter`, `debye_phonon_screening_starter`).
   - Surface structured `StatMechError::InvalidChemicalPotential` guidance and `SimulationFramework` rollback/clamp guards when $\mu \ge E$ in bosonic `BoseEinstein` mode without emitting `NaN` plot samples, and share deterministic `.csv`, `.json`, `.svg` dataset/figure exports plus `.md` and `.csv` physics datasheets across all three solid-state plot tools.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Finite-temperature `IsotropicBCSModel` reproduces the universal weak-coupling ratio $2\Delta(0)/(k_B T_c) \approx 3.528$, recovers the condensed branch from a `0.0 eV` seed below $T_c$, and drives $\Delta(T) \to 0.0\text{ eV}$ and $G_{NS}(V)/G_{NN} \to 1.0$ above $T_c$ with zero heap allocations inside `#[verified_engine::verified]` gap-iteration and tunneling hot loops under `VerifiedAllocator`.
- **Positive**: `calculate_occupancy_ev` evaluates $f_{\text{FD}}(0.10\text{ eV}, 0\text{ eV}, 300\text{ K}) \approx 0.0205$ accurately in CODATA SI units while `canonical_thermodynamics_ev` prevents Boltzmann partition underflow for arbitrarily shifted positive energy ladders.
- **Neutral**: Anisotropic multi-band 3D Fermi surfaces, Eliashberg strong-coupling integral equations, and ab-initio DFT band solvers remain out of scope so interactive desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` renders stay local-first and sub-millisecond.
