# ADR 20261123: Zero-Allocation SDS Radiance & LoraHub Simplex Solvers, Self-Calibration Export, and Interactive AI Playgrounds

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0021](0021-zero-allocation-grpo-advantage-normalization.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), and [ADR 0041](0041-volumetric-field-studio-zero-alloc-3d-kernels.md))

## Context
[`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/mod.rs) previously defined traits (`NeRFModel`, `DifferentiableNeRF`, `TextEncoder`, `DiffusionModel`) whose render loop (`render_image_with_rng`) locked global heap allocations via `verified_engine::allocator::lock_allocations()`, yet shipped no concrete zero-allocation volumetric model or score prior outside unit test stubs. Similarly, [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/lorahub/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/lorahub/mod.rs) implemented linear adapter merging and `L1RegularizationStrategy`, but lacked the gradient-free Nelder-Mead black-box simplex optimizer described in [`papers/lorahub.tex`](../../papers/lorahub.tex) (`arXiv:2307.13269`), while [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/training.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/training.rs) kept `calculate_target_distribution` private and [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml) misattributed `self_calibration_paper.tex` to `domain_physics` due to a stray citation in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/optical_motion/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/optical_motion/mod.rs). Finally, none of GRPO alignment, self-calibration confidence distillation, LoraHub simplex composition, or 3D Score Distillation Sampling had interactive playgrounds in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/mod.rs).

## Decision
1. **Self-Calibration Public Target Distribution & Documentation Repairs (`domain_ai`, `domain_applied`)**:
   - Export `calculate_target_distribution` publicly in [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/training.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/training.rs) and [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/self_calibration/mod.rs) with runnable doctests, correct the `self_calibration` description in [`crates/domain_ai/README.md`](../../crates/domain_ai/README.md), and document `beta` accurately as the active reference-policy KL penalty weight in [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/grpo/formulas.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/grpo/formulas.rs).
2. **Zero-Allocation Nelder-Mead Simplex Solver (`domain_applied::lorahub`)**:
   - Add [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/lorahub/simplex.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/lorahub/simplex.rs) providing `NelderMeadConfig`, `NelderMeadSimplex`, `SimplexStepOutcome`, and `SimplexOptimizationSummary` over pre-allocated flat vertex, order, centroid, and trial buffers (`initialize_in_place`, `step_in_place`, `optimize_in_place`), wired into [`LoraEnsemble`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/lorahub/ensemble.rs) (`step_simplex_with_l1`, `optimize_weights_in_place`) under `L1RegularizationStrategy`.
3. **Concrete Zero-Allocation Volumetric NeRF & Closed-Form Score Priors (`domain_ai::sds`)**:
   - Add [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/models.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/models.rs) and [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/priors.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/sds/priors.rs) implementing `AnalyticalVoxelNeRF` (stack-only trilinear `NeRFModel::query` passing `lock_allocations()` and analytical volumetric transmittance VJP `DifferentiableNeRF::backward`), `PromptPrototypeEncoder` (`TextEncoder`), and `AnalyticalScorePrior` (`DiffusionModel` via closed-form Tweedie Gaussian diffusion score estimation).
4. **Interactive `AiTab` Playgrounds & Traceability Link Repairs (`math_explorer_gui`, `traceability.toml`)**:
   - Register [`GrpoAlignmentLabTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/grpo_alignment_lab/mod.rs), [`LoraHubSimplexTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/lorahub_simplex/mod.rs), and 3D [`SdsNeRfDistillationTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/sds_nerf_distillation/mod.rs) (bounded to `<= 32x32` rays and `<= 24` stratified samples per ray on `Plot3DViewport` with `PlaybackTransport`, `AccessKit` focusable canvases, and WCAG 2.2 AA `SemanticPalette` / `colormap` tokens) in `AiTab`.
   - Remove the stray citation in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/optical_motion/mod.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/optical_motion/mod.rs), map `self_calibration_paper.tex` exclusively to `domain_ai` in [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml), and wire `grpo.tex`, `self_calibration_paper.tex`, and `lorahub.tex` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/theory_links.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/theory_links.rs).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: GRPO group rollouts, Nelder-Mead simplex steps, and `AnalyticalVoxelNeRF::query` ray integration execute with zero dynamic heap allocations under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, while `AnalyticalVoxelNeRF` monotonically reduces target reconstruction MSE under both `AdamOptimizer` and `SgdOptimizer`.
- **Positive**: Native and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` users can interactively inspect GRPO surrogate clipping, self-calibration KL distillation, LoraHub L1 sparsity contours, and 3D SDS radiance sculpting with full starter presets, URL round-trips, deferred figure exports, and verified academic paper links.
- **Neutral**: Real-time main-thread SDS ray grids are intentionally bounded to `32x32` pixels and `24` samples per ray to sustain responsive browser frame budgets without GPU compute shaders.
