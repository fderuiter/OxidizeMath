# ADR 20261124: Tier 0 `#![no_std]` Mathematical Scalars (`math_scalars`), Montgomery Prime Fields, Stack Galois/p-adic Algebras, and Complex Linear Solvers

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0016](0016-standardize-crate-documentation-suite.md), [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), [ADR 20261123: Checked Companion APIs](20261123-checked-apis-and-typed-domain-errors.md), and [ADR 20261123: `#![no_std]` Linear Algebra Engine (`engine_linalg`)](20261123-no-std-engine-linalg-and-solver-unification.md))

## Context
Scalar traits, arbitrary-precision numbers, hypercomplex rotations, and modular/p-adic arithmetic across OxidizeMath previously suffered from six structural gaps:
1. **Duplicated Ordered `Scalar` Contracts Blocking Complex Solvers**: `engine_linalg::scalar::Scalar` and `math_commons::math_kernel::types::Scalar` independently duplicated ~180 lines of `f32`/`f64` trait glue while bundling total ordering (`PartialOrd`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `atan2`), preventing `Complex<T>` from participating in generic LU, pivoted Householder QR, or restarted GMRES solvers.
2. **Precision Loss and Raw Tuple Access in `pure_math::math_types`**: `Float::with_val(prec, v)` converted `f64` via `FBig::try_from(v)` without applying `with_precision(prec)`, silently truncating caller-requested bit precisions above 53 bits, and `Rational` lacked exact `Integer` numerator/denominator accessors, forcing algorithmic-information callers (`geometry.rs`, `fractran_factor.rs`) to reach into `.0.numerator()`.
3. **No `#![no_std]` Fixed-Width Fallback When `alloc` Is Disabled**: Bare-metal and zero-allocation callers had no stack-allocated 256-bit integer fallback (`U256`, `I256`) when `dashu` (`alloc`) was disabled.
4. **Fragmented Quaternion Rotation Representations**: `domain_ai::ai::gaussian_splatting::structs::Gaussian3D` stored `nalgebra::UnitQuaternion<f64>` and manually shuffled `[q.w, q.i, q.j, q.k]` into raw `[f32; 4]` arrays in `oxidize_core::mesh::export_splat_ply::GaussianSplatPly`, which maintained a duplicate `normalize_quat` helper.
5. **Signed Composite-Permissive `Fp<const P: i64>` and Overflow-Prone `modular_fp`**: `pure_math::algebra::fields::Fp<const P: i64>` accepted composite moduli at compile time, panicked on zero inversion via `assert_ne!`, and `elliptic_curves::modular_fp` duplicated `i64` modular helpers limited to small signed bounds.
6. **Heap-Bound Polynomials and Missing Stack $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ / $\mathbb{Q}_p$ Engines**: `pure_math::algebra::Polynomial` required `Vec<T>` without `EuclideanDomain` division, and `number_theory::ambs` / `elliptic_curves` lacked stack-allocated fixed-precision $p$-adic numbers (`Qp<P, N>`) with Newton-Hensel root lifting.

## Decision
1. **Create Tier 0 `#![no_std]` [`crates/00_foundation/math_scalars`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/lib.rs)**:
   - Configure optional `alloc` (`dep:dashu` with `default-features = false`) and `std` features (`default = []`), traced to `papers/advanced_linear_algebra.tex` via [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml).
   - Provide `alloc`-gated `BigInt`, `BigRational` (with exact `numerator()` / `denominator()` accessors), and `BigFloat` (`try_with_prec(prec, val)` preserving exact significand bit precision while rejecting `prec == 0`, `NaN`, and `±Infinity` with [`ScalarError`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/error.rs)), re-exported in [`pure_math::math_types`](../../crates/pure_math/src/math_types.rs) alongside fixed `Float::with_val` / `Float::try_with_prec` and `Rational::numerator` / `Rational::denominator`.
   - Provide zero-allocation stack-resident 4-limb little-endian [`U256`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/uint256.rs) and two's-complement [`I256`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/int256.rs) with checked and Euclidean division/remainder (`div_rem_euclid`, `checked_div_euclid`, `checked_rem_euclid`) even when `alloc` is disabled.
2. **Split `FieldScalar` and `RealScalar` + Flat `#[repr(C)]` Cayley-Dickson Algebras**:
   - Split [`FieldScalar`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/traits.rs) (unordered division-field contract with `AssocReal: RealScalar`, `conj`, `abs`, `abs_sq`, `checked_inv`) from [`RealScalar`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/traits.rs) (ordered real transcendentals backed by `libm`), deduplicating [`engine_linalg::scalar`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_linalg/src/scalar.rs) and [`math_commons::math_kernel::types`](../../crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs).
   - Implement flat `#[repr(C)]` [`Complex<T>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/complex.rs) (with Smith overflow-safe division/inversion), [`Quaternion<T>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/quaternion.rs), [`UnitQuaternion<T>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/unit_quaternion.rs), and [`Octonion<T>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/octonion.rs).
   - Generalize [`engine_linalg`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_linalg/src/lib.rs) (`lu`, `qr`, `krylov::gmres_solve` with Hermitian Arnoldi inner products and unitary complex Givens rotations, `norms`, `sparse`, and `SolverWorkspace`) to `T: FieldScalar`, enabling zero-allocation `Complex32` / `Complex64` linear solves, and unify 3D rotations on `UnitQuaternion` across [`Gaussian3D`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/gaussian_splatting/structs.rs) and [`GaussianSplatPly`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/mesh/export_splat_ply.rs).
3. **Compile-Time Montgomery [`Fp<const P: u64>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/fp.rs), [`StackPoly`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/poly.rs), [`FpExt`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/galois.rs), and [`Qp<const P: u64, const N: usize>`](../../crates/00_foundation/math_scalars/src/padic.rs)**:
   - Validate `P` at compile time via deterministic 12-witness `const fn is_prime_u64` (exact for all `u64`, up to the largest 64-bit prime `18_446_744_073_709_551_557`, with an explicit `P == 2` path), 128-bit carry-safe Montgomery REDC multiplication, and fallible `checked_inverse() -> Result<Self, ScalarError>`, replacing `Fp<const P: i64>` in [`pure_math::algebra::fields`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/algebra/fields.rs) and retiring duplicate modular helpers in [`modular_fp.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/elliptic_curves/modular_fp.rs).
   - Provide stack-backed `StackPoly<T, CAP>` with `EuclideanDomain` division, extended GCD (`xgcd`), and Galois quotient fields `FpExt<P, DEG, M>` ($\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$), plus `EuclideanDomain` division on `pure_math::algebra::Polynomial<T: Field>`.
   - Provide stack-allocated $p$-adic numbers `Qp<P, N>` with canonical base-$P$ digits, valuations, units, arithmetic, and Newton-Hensel root lifting (`hensel_lift_root`), wired into [`ambs_padic.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/number_theory/ambs_padic.rs) and [`elliptic_curves`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/elliptic_curves/mod.rs).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `math_scalars` and `engine_linalg` compile cleanly under `--no-default-features` and on `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, execute `U256`, `I256`, hypercomplex, `Fp`, `StackPoly`, `FpExt`, `Qp`, and `Complex64` LU/QR/GMRES operations with zero heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`, and reject composite prime-field moduli at compile time.
- **Positive**: Eliminates ~230 lines of duplicated scalar traits, modular helpers, and quaternion normalizers while removing raw `.0` tuple coupling in `pure_math` algorithmic-information modules.
- **Neutral**: Workspace member count increases from 23 to 24 crates, recorded across governance manifests, architecture guides, `Verification_Certificate.json`, and `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`.
