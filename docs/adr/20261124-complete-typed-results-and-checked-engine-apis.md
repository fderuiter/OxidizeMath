# ADR 20261124: Complete Typed `Result` Coverage, `QuantumError`, and `#![no_std]` Checked Engine APIs

## Status
Accepted (2026-11-24; extends [ADR 20261123](20261123-checked-apis-and-typed-domain-errors.md) and [ADR 20261123](20261123-nostd-diffeq-and-pde-engines.md))

## Context
Following [ADR 20261123](20261123-checked-apis-and-typed-domain-errors.md) and the extraction of Tier 2 `#![no_std]` differential-equation and PDE engines ([ADR 20261123](20261123-nostd-diffeq-and-pde-engines.md)), an API baseline audit identified three remaining gaps in typed error coverage and non-panicking solver entrypoints:

1. **Five Residual Untyped `Result` Signatures**: Public library items in [`crates/oxidize_core/src/traceability/debt_floors.rs`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/traceability/debt_floors.rs) (`parse_density_floors_toml`), [`crates/domain_ai/src/ai/reinforcement_learning/maze.rs`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/ai/reinforcement_learning/maze.rs) (`SculptedGridMdp::apply_cells_u8`), [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs) (`MriVolume::new`), [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/mask.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/mask.rs) (`CellMask2D::set_roles_slice`), and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/hydrogenic.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/hydrogenic.rs) (`OrbitalQuantumNumbers::new`) still returned `Result<_, String>` or `Result<_, &'static str>`.
2. **`#[cfg(feature = "std")]`-Gated Stencil Constructors**: `StencilOperators::{try_new, try_new_2d, try_new_3d}` and `FusedStencilStepper::{try_new, try_new_2d, try_new_3d}` in [`crates/engine_pde`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/lib.rs) were gated behind `#[cfg(feature = "std")]` even though [`MathError`](../../crates/math_commons/src/error.rs) is unconditionally available in `#![no_std]`.
3. **Panicking Workspace, PDE/SPDE, and SDE Entrypoints**: `SolverWorkspace::take_slice` in [`crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs), Fourier/stencil SPDE steppers and FFT/noise helpers in [`crates/engine_pde`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/lib.rs), SDE/Langevin integrators (`EulerMaruyamaSolver`, `MilsteinSolver`, `Sriw1Solver`, `BaoabLangevinSolver`, `eval_ito_effective_drift`) in [`crates/engine_diffeq`](../../crates/engine_diffeq/src/lib.rs), and `QuantumState::probability_density_in_place` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/types.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/types.rs) asserted on caller slice lengths, workspace capacities, radix-2 grid dimensions, or parameter domains without non-panicking `try_*` companions.

## Decision
1. **Extend `MathError` and `SolverWorkspace` for Zero-Allocation `#![no_std]` Validation**:
   - Add stack-allocated `WorkspaceTooSmall { required, available }` and `InvalidRadix2GridSize { size, min }` variants to [`MathError`](../../crates/math_commons/src/error.rs) with human-readable `Display` and `Diagnostic` metadata.
   - Add `SolverWorkspace::try_take_slice` in [`crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs) that leaves the bump slice unchanged on `Err`, and delegate `take_slice` to it.
2. **Eliminate Every Remaining Untyped Library `Result` Signature**:
   - Migrate `parse_density_floors_toml` to [`DensityFloorsError`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/traceability/debt_floors.rs), `SculptedGridMdp::apply_cells_u8` to [`AIError`](../../crates/domain_ai/src/error.rs), `MriVolume::new` to [`EngineeringError`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/error.rs), and `CellMask2D::set_roles_slice` to [`ReactionDiffusionError`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/reaction_diffusion/mod.rs).
   - Introduce stack-allocated [`QuantumError`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/types.rs) (`InvalidPrincipalQuantumNumber`, `InvalidOrbitalQuantumNumber`, `InvalidMagneticQuantumNumber`, `StateLengthMismatch`) implementing `thiserror::Error` and `Diagnostic`, wire `PhysicsError::Quantum(QuantumError)` into [`domain_physics::error`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/error.rs) and [`math_explorer::error::Error`](../../math_explorer/src/error.rs), migrate `OrbitalQuantumNumbers::new` to `Result<Self, QuantumError>`, and add `QuantumState::try_probability_density_in_place`.
3. **Provide Unconditional `#![no_std]` `try_*` Companions Across `engine_pde` and `engine_diffeq`**:
   - Remove `#[cfg(feature = "std")]` from `StencilOperators::try_new*` and `FusedStencilStepper::try_new*`.
   - Add `SemiImplicitFourierStepper::{try_new_1d, try_new_2d}` and checked Fourier/stencil SPDE steppers, radix-2 FFT/dealiasing (`try_fft_1d_inplace`, `try_fft_2d_inplace`, `try_dealias_1d_spectrum`, `try_dealias_2d_spectrum`), and noise injection (`try_inject_*`) in [`crates/engine_pde`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/lib.rs), splitting checked Fourier and stencil SPDE steppers into [`crates/engine_pde/src/fourier_stepper/checked.rs`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/fourier_stepper/checked.rs) and [`crates/engine_pde/src/spde_stencil/checked.rs`](../../crates/engine_pde/src/spde_stencil/checked.rs) to stay well within the 500-line file ceiling.
   - Add `BaoabLangevinSolver::{try_new, try_step}`, `EulerMaruyamaSolver::{try_step, try_step_with_increments}`, `MilsteinSolver::{try_step, try_step_with_increments}`, `Sriw1Solver::{try_step, try_step_with_correlated_increments}`, and `try_eval_ito_effective_drift` in [`crates/engine_diffeq`](../../crates/engine_diffeq/src/lib.rs), validating slice dimensions, positive finite step sizes, and scratch capacities up front before mutating state or RNG streams.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Zero public library `Result` signatures in [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json) or [`crates/math_commons/src/primitives.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/primitives.rs) return `String`, `&'static str`, or `()`, and zero `#[allow(clippy::result_unit_err)]` suppressions exist anywhere in the workspace.
- **Positive**: All `#![no_std]` PDE, SPDE, SDE, Langevin, and quantum kernels expose zero-allocation `try_*` entrypoints verified under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified` while preserving exact IEEE-754 results and existing panicking `new`/`step` ergonomics.
- **Neutral**: `OrbitalQuantumNumbers::new` and the four other migrated `Result` return types update their error type in `api_baseline.json` with downstream GUI call sites updated in lockstep.
