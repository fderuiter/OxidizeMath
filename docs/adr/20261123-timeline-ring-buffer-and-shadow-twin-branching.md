# ADR 20261123: Timeline Ring-Buffer Scrubbing and Lockstep Shadow-Twin Branching

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0032](0032-high-contrast-focus-selection-and-raster-tokens.md))

## Context
Forward time-stepping tools across `math_explorer_gui` previously offered only destructive `Reset` ($t = 0$), `Play/Pause`, and single forward `Step` (`Period`) actions. Exploring transient interventions—such as mid-outbreak epidemiological lockdowns (`SirTool`), $\Delta x = 10^{-5}$ butterfly perturbations on 3D strange attractors (`AttractorPlotter`), mid-relaxation $T_1 / T_2 / B_1$ shifts (`MriTool`), or mid-wave obstacle sculpting (`WaveUnified`, `LbmUnified`, and `HodgkinHuxleyUnified`)—forced users to restart from $t = 0$ and mentally compare sequential runs without quantitative divergence telemetry. Supporting non-destructive timeline scrubbing and side-by-side counterfactual trajectories introduces five architectural constraints:
1. **Bounded Preallocated Memory**: Arbitrary checkpoint retention across 2D PDE fields or multi-thousand-step ODE histories would risk unbounded heap growth unless capped strictly at $\le 2\text{ MiB}$ (`MAX_TIMELINE_RING_BYTES`) per tool instance.
2. **Zero-Allocation Solver Inner Loops**: Both primary and unperturbed shadow-twin steppers must execute with `0` dynamic heap allocations inside `VerifiedEngine::run_verified` (`VerifiedAllocator`) and maintain bit-identical step parity between native background workers ([ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md)) and cooperative `wasm32` single-threaded slices.
3. **Accessible Transport & Quantitative Divergence Telemetry**: Scrubbing, step-back (`Comma` symmetric with `Period`), and branch forking must satisfy WCAG 2.2 AA (`SemanticPalette` contrast $\ge 4.5:1$ text / $\ge 3.0:1$ graphical strokes, ARIA live-region status announcements) while reporting instantaneous $L^2$ distance, peak absolute delta, cumulative trapezoidal area delta, and empirical log-divergence slope ($\lambda_{\text{emp}}$).
4. **Compact Shareable URL State**: `InteractiveTool::save_state` must remain parameter-only within `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4,096` bytes, [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md)), never serializing transient ring-buffer checkpoints or shadow histories into shareable URLs.
5. **Reproducible Multi-Format Exports**: Dataset (`CSV`, `JSON`) and figure (`SVG`, `PNG`, [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md)) exports must preserve both baseline shadow series and counterfactual branch series alongside structured branch provenance from $t_k$.

## Decision
1. **`TimelineRingBuffer`, `BranchDivergenceAccumulator`, and `BranchDivergenceHud`**:
   - Introduce [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/timeline_branch.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/timeline_branch.rs) (with [`hud.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/timeline_branch/hud.rs) and [`exports.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/timeline_branch/exports.rs)) providing a preallocated circular checkpoint store bounded at `2,097,152` bytes (`2 MiB`) per tool instance, O(1) modular slot overwrite without reallocation, checkpoint lookup/truncation at $t_k > 0$, online divergence integration ($\|u - u_{\text{shadow}}\|_2$, $\|u - u_{\text{shadow}}\|_\infty$, $\int \|u - u_{\text{shadow}}\|_2\,dt$, and least-squares $\lambda_{\text{emp}}$), and WCAG-compliant HUD/ARIA helpers (`announce_branch_forked`, `announce_branch_cleared`).
2. **Unified Transport, Framework Delegation, and Worker Commands**:
   - Extend [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/playback.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/playback.rs) and [`playback_timeline.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/playback_timeline.rs) with `PlaybackTransport::show_with_timeline` (`⏮` step-back button, `,` keyboard shortcut, `Timeline` scrubber slider, and `⑂ Fork Branch` toggle), delegated via optional `InteractiveTool` hooks in [`timeline_hooks.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/timeline_hooks.rs).
   - Extend [`math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/state.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/state.rs) with `SimCommand::{StepBack, ScrubToStep, ForkBranch, ClearBranch}` and `UnifiedModel` zero-allocation `checkpoint_field_len` / `branch_checkpoint_stride` / `write_branch_checkpoint` / `restore_branch_checkpoint` / `fork_shadow_twin` hooks in [`math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/unified/branching.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/unified/branching.rs), keeping primary and shadow-twin stepping lockstep on the single worker / cooperative `wasm32` slice without spawning extra threads.
3. **Domain Tool Rollout & Perceptual Overlays**:
   - **Raster & Curve `UnifiedSimTool` Models**: Implement full checkpointing and perceptual signed diverging difference heatmaps (`Diff (u - u_shadow)` with colorblind-safe blue-neutral-amber `SemanticPalette` ramp) for [`WaveUnified`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/wave_unified.rs) (backed by two-level Störmer-Verlet checkpoint helpers in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/wave_2d/masks.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/wave_2d/masks.rs)), [`LbmUnified`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann_branch.rs) (backed by D2Q9 distribution checkpoint helpers in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/state.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/state.rs)), and [`HodgkinHuxleyUnified`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/neuroscience/hodgkin_huxley.rs).
   - **Compartmental, Chaotic 3D, and Bloch ODE Tools**: Implement preallocated ring-buffer scrubbing, dashed baseline + solid counterfactual overlays, and zero-allocation lockstep shadow twins in [`SirTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/epidemiology/sir/branch.rs), [`AttractorPlotter`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/attractors/branch.rs) (3D `Scene3DBuilder` dashed/solid polylines + $\Delta x = 10^{-5}$ butterfly perturbation), and [`MriTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/mri/branch.rs).
4. **Structured Branch Provenance in Dataset & Figure Exports**:
   - Augment [`export_dataset.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_dataset.rs) and [`export_figure.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_figure.rs) so `CSV` and `JSON` exports include shadow series and `Branch Metadata` tables, `SVG` figures embed `<desc id="oxidizemath-branch-metadata">`, and `PNG` figures inject a valid CRC-32 `tEXt` chunk (`OxidizeMath-Branch`) before `IEND`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can scrub backward to any buffered $t_k > 0$ without resetting to $t = 0$, fork unperturbed shadow twins in lockstep on both native workers and `wasm32`, inspect live $L^2$/peak/area/$\lambda_{\text{emp}}$ divergence metrics with screen-reader announcements, and export publication-ready comparative datasets and figures while maintaining `0` inner-loop heap allocations and `< 4 KiB` URL presets.
- **Neutral**: Tools that do not opt into `timeline_transport_state` retain the compact transport bar and baseline single-trajectory export pipelines with zero memory overhead.
