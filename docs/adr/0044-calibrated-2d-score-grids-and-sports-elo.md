# ADR 0044: Calibrated 2D Score Grids and Venue-Adjusted Sports Elo Engine

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0037](0037-glicko2-frozen-period-tournament-simulator.md) and [ADR 0042](0042-hierarchical-umbrella-errors-and-diagnostic-bus-helpers.md))

## Context
[`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/mod.rs) previously exported Bayesian [`glicko2`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/glicko2/mod.rs), [`kelly`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/kelly/mod.rs), and univariate [`zip_regression`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/zip_regression/mod.rs), leaving two gaps for quantitative sports rating and match-pricing workflows across native desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown`:
1. **Missing Venue-Adjusted Sports Elo & Margin Scaling**: `glicko2` models symmetric head-to-head ratings without venue offsets, win-margin (Margin of Victory) autocorrelation damping, between-season mean regression, or direct converters to fair moneyline [`Odds`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/kelly/core.rs) and point spreads.
2. **Uncorrelated Univariate Goal Models & Missing Spread/Total Markets**: Independent Poisson goal models underprice low-scoring draws in association football (e.g., 26.8397% vs 30.1531% — a 12.35% relative shift at $\lambda = 1.35$, $\mu = 1.15$, $\rho = -0.13$) and lacked a bounded zero-allocation 2D joint probability matrix (`ScoreGrid`) to price `1X2`, Draw-No-Bet, BTTS, and whole/half/quarter Asian handicaps and Over/Under totals.

## Decision
1. **Add `pure_math::statistics::elo`**:
   - Define validated [`EloRating`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/elo/core.rs), `Venue`, `MarginScaling` (`None`, `Logarithmic`, `Autocorrelated`), and `EloConfig` (default scale `400.0`, $K = 20.0$, home advantage `65.0`) with `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` boundary-checked `Deserialize` implementations.
   - Implement base-10 logistic expected scores, autocorrelation-adjusted win-margin scaling (`ln(|delta_goals| + 1) * (2.2 / (|delta_r_adj| * 0.001 + 2.2))`), and seasonal regression (`(1 - theta) * r + theta * mean_rating`) in [`elo/update.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/elo/update.rs).
   - Provide order-invariant frozen-period (`evaluate_elo_batch_into`, `evaluate_elo_batch`) and sequential (`evaluate_elo_sequential_into`, `evaluate_elo_sequential`) multi-team evaluators in [`elo/batch.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/elo/batch.rs) plus moneyline `Odds`, `UnitInterval`, and linear spread converters in [`elo/markets.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/elo/markets.rs).
2. **Add `pure_math::statistics::match_models`**:
   - Implement stack-backed [`ScoreGrid`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/grid.rs) over `0..=max_goals` (`DEFAULT_MAX_GOALS = 15`, `MAX_GOALS_LIMIT = 31`, `[f64; 1024]`) indexed strictly via [`flatten_2d_index`](../../crates/math_commons/src/math_kernel/types.rs), recording `unnormalized_mass`, `tail_mass = (1.0 - unnormalized_mass).max(0.0)`, and optional normalization with zero heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`.
   - Implement log-space Maher independent Poisson ([`poisson.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/poisson.rs)), Karlis-Ntzoufras bivariate Poisson with additive shared-shock and marginal-preserving constructors and overflow-free log-space recurrence ([`bivariate_poisson.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/bivariate_poisson.rs)), and Dixon-Coles low-score $\tau$ correlation with exact $\rho$ feasibility bounds ([`dixon_coles.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/dixon_coles.rs)).
   - Implement exact `1X2`, Draw-No-Bet, BTTS, Asian Handicap, and Over/Under total pricers across whole, half, and split quarter lines in [`markets.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/markets.rs), alongside time-decayed `fit_dixon_coles_mle` with `exp(-xi * age)` weights, sum-to-zero attack identifiability, and [`optimization.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/optimization.rs)-compatible bounded gradient updates in [`calibration.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/statistics/match_models/calibration.rs).
3. **Wire `EloError` and `MatchModelError` Across Umbrella & Facade Layers**:
   - Define `EloError` and `MatchModelError` in [`crates/pure_math/src/error.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/error.rs), implement `Diagnostic` in [`submodule_diagnostics.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/error/submodule_diagnostics.rs), wire both into [`PureMathError`](../../crates/pure_math/src/error/umbrella.rs), re-export them through [`math_explorer/src/error.rs`](../../math_explorer/src/error.rs), and regenerate [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Sports Elo batch evaluations, 2D score-grid construction, and exact whole/half/quarter market pricing execute deterministically with zero dynamic heap allocations on both native and WebAssembly targets.
- **Positive**: Correlated score grids eliminate independent-Poisson low-score draw mispricing while exposing truncated `tail_mass` diagnostics and boundary-validated `serde` payloads.
- **Neutral**: Public additions to `pure_math::statistics::{elo, match_models}` and `math_explorer::error` expand the tracked public API baseline by two statistics modules and two leaf error enums.
