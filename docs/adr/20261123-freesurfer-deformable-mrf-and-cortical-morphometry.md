# ADR 20261123: Zero-Allocation FreeSurfer Cortical Surface Physics, Potts MRF Segmentation, and 3D Cortical Morphometry Studio

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0018](0018-uniform-spatial-grid-cortical-thickness.md), [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), and [ADR 0041](0041-volumetric-field-studio-zero-alloc-3d-kernels.md))

## Context
[`papers/freesurfer.tex`](../../papers/freesurfer.tex) specifies cortical surface reconstruction via membrane tension ($\alpha$) and thin-plate bending ($\beta$) internal energy $E_{\text{int}}$, Gaussian-smoothed volumetric MRI intensity gradient attraction $E_{\text{ext}} = -\sum_i \|\nabla(G_\sigma * I)(x_i)\|^2$, Bayesian tissue classification coupled to a 3D Potts Markov Random Field (MRF) spatial smoothness prior, symmetric white-to-pial cortical thickness maps, and multi-subject General Linear Model (GLM) $t$-contrasts. However, [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/mod.rs) previously implemented `Surface` as a disconnected `Vec<Point3<f64>>` point cloud, ignored `beta` in `internal_energy` while allocating temporary `DVector` heap vectors per edge, ignored `sigma` in `external_energy`, displaced vertices by constant scalar `learning_rate` along `[1, 1, 1]` in `evolve_surface`, returned unnormalized single-voxel Gaussian densities in `bayesian_classification`, rebuilt `SpatialGrid` for every vertex when evaluating full-mesh cortical thickness, and had no interactive visualization in `math_explorer_gui`.

## Decision
1. **Indexed Triangular `Surface`, Zero-Allocation Deformable Physics, and `MriVolume` (`domain_applied`)**:
   - Extend [`Surface`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs) with `faces: Vec<[usize; 3]>` (`Surface::new`, `Surface::from_vertices`) while preserving compatibility with existing public function signatures (`internal_energy`, `external_energy`, `evolve_surface`, `cortical_thickness`, `bayesian_classification`, `estimate_beta`, `t_statistic`).
   - Implement zero-allocation chunked (`SURFACE_CHUNK = 512`) `internal_energy` combining undirected triangle-edge tension $\frac{\alpha}{2}\sum \|x_i - x_j\|^2$ and 1-ring discrete umbrella-Laplacian bending $\frac{\beta}{2}\sum \|\Delta x_i\|^2$ (falling back cleanly to sequential polyline pairs when `faces` is empty).
   - Add [`MriVolume`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs) (`sample_trilinear`, separable `smoothed_intensity`, central-difference `smoothed_gradient`), `external_energy_volume`, normal-stepping `evolve_surface` (skipping degenerate zero-length normals), and trust-region-bounded `evolve_surface_deformable` combining normal external gradient descent with discrete Laplacian ($\alpha$) and biharmonic ($\beta$) regularization under zero heap allocations.
2. **Single-Build Batch `cortical_thickness_map` and Red-Black Potts MRF Segmentation (`domain_applied`)**:
   - Add [`cortical_thickness_map`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/thickness.rs) building `SpatialGrid::from_surface` once for `white_surface` and once for `pial_surface` across the entire mesh.
   - Add [`TissueClass`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/segmentation.rs), stack-backed `normalized_posteriors_into` / `normalized_posteriors` (falling back to uniform $1/K$ probabilities when likelihoods underflow to zero), `mrf_voxel_posteriors`, and zero-allocation in-place `mrf_segment_volume` using red-black (checkerboard) 6-connected Iterated Conditional Modes (ICM).
3. **Interactive 3D `CorticalMorphometryTool` and Watertight `ColoredMesh` Export (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Register [`CorticalMorphometryTool`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/neuroscience/cortical_morphometry.rs) in `NeuroscienceTab` (`domain: "neuroscience"`, linked to `freesurfer.tex` in [`theory_links.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/framework/theory_links.rs)) alongside three `StarterPresetMetadata` cards (`cortical_sulcal_thickness`, `cortical_glm_atrophy_contrast`, `cortical_mrf_deformable_recon`).
   - Render folded and continuously inflated (`0.0..=1.0`) welded-pole genus-0 white and pial cortical hemispheres via `Plot3DViewport` and `Scene3DBuilder` using `heatmap_color_high_contrast` for `cortical_thickness_map`, cohort GLM `t_statistic`, Potts MRF gray-matter posteriors, and sulcal depth, with `FiniteStateGuard` rollback/clamping, non-panicking status banners on singular $X^T X$ designs, and watertight outward-oriented `ColoredMesh` exports (`STL`/`PLY`/`OBJ`).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Hot-path deformable surface energies, surface evolution steps, and 6-connected Potts MRF sweeps execute with zero heap allocations verified by `VerifiedAllocator`, while `cortical_thickness_map` eliminates $O(V)$ redundant `SpatialGrid` rebuilds.
- **Positive**: Desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` users can unfold buried sulcal pathology, compare Potts MRF denoising against independent maximum-likelihood classification, inspect cohort GLM $t$-statistics, and export watertight per-vertex colored 3D cortical solids.
- **Neutral**: Full NIfTI/MGZ volumetric file ingestion and topological genus-fixing surgery remain out of scope so all browser presets remain stateless and compact (`< 1 KiB`) within `URL_JSON_BOUNDS`.
