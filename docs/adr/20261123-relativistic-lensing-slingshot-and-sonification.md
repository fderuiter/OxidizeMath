# ADR 20261123: Relativistic Gravitational Lensing, Canvas Slingshot Gestures, and Redshift Sonification Studio

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0035](0035-iterative-ast-glsl-transpiler-and-shader-exports.md), [ADR 0040](0040-dual-backend-web-audio-sonification.md), [ADR 0040](0040-galactic-dynamics-and-astrophysics-tab.md), [ADR 0044](0044-geodesic-engine-and-relativity-tab.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
[`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/relativity/`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/relativity/mod.rs) and [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/astrophysics/`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/astrophysics/mod.rs) previously exposed slider-driven single-ray trajectories and passive pre-seeded galactic tracer clouds without direct canvas launch gestures, gravitational-lensing optical cameras, or Web Audio sonification. In [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity/kerr.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity/kerr.rs), equatorial Kerr telemetry fell back to static Schwarzschild clock dilation and effective potentials, omitting `frame_dragging_omega` and `ergosphere_radius` effects, while [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/observer.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/observer.rs) lacked covariant `FourVector` Lorentz boosts and arbitrary-angle relativistic Doppler factors. Finally, both tabs lacked standalone WebGL2 / Shadertoy `.frag` fragment shader exports and polyphonic Doppler / synchrotron audio synthesis.

## Decision
1. **Covariant Observer Boosts, Exact Equatorial Kerr Observables, and Deflection-Table Lensing (`domain_physics` & `pure_math`)**:
   - Extend [`FourVector`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/observer.rs) with `lorentz_boost` and `relativistic_doppler_factor` verifying Minkowski interval invariance $\eta_{\mu\nu} x^\mu x^\nu$.
   - Extend [`KerrEquatorialMetric`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity/kerr.rs) with exact Boyer-Lindquist `clock_dilation`, `zamo_clock_dilation`, `effective_potential`, `photon_orbit_radius`, and `critical_impact_parameter`, pairing with `frame_dragging_omega` and `ergosphere_radius`.
   - Add `GeodesicSystem::integrate_bounded_rk4` in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/geodesic.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/geodesic.rs) and [`DeflectionTable`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity/lensing.rs) in `domain_physics`, pre-allocating signed impact-parameter bins across Schwarzschild ($b_{\text{crit}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} R_s$ Einstein ring), spinning Kerr (asymmetric prograde/retrograde D-shadow), and Morris-Thorne wormhole ($l > 0$ and $l < 0$ two-sheet portal) metrics for $O(1)$ zero-allocation ray lookups.
   - Pre-allocate `SLINGSHOT_RING_CAPACITY = 8` `OrbitParticleKind::SlingshotProbe` slots, `launch_tracer_in_place`, and `sample_surface_brightness` on [`GalacticDiskSystem`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/astrophysics/orbits_slingshot.rs).
2. **Modular Relativity Playground, Cross-Domain Lensing Camera, and `SpatialBrushKit` Galactic Slingshots (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Split [`GeodesicPlaygroundTool`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/relativity/playground.rs) into focused submodules (`sim.rs`, `sim_integrators.rs`, `slingshot.rs`, `lensing_view.rs`, `lensing_shader.rs`, `ui.rs`, `viewport_3d.rs`) supporting `Orbit`, `Slingshot`, and `PhotonFan` (up to `16` simultaneous pre-allocated geodesics) modes, locking `Camera3D` rotation via `dragging_callout` outside `Orbit` mode and handling `Arrow`, `Space`, and `Enter` keyboard launches via `lock_canvas_arrow_keys`.
   - Add a 2D gravitational lensing camera warping `GalacticDiskSystem`, deep-field stargrids, and `#[cfg(feature = "biology")]` `LeniaEngine` soliton portals (with a procedural fallback when `biology` is disabled), alongside `export_raster_image` and standalone WebGL2 (`#version 300 es`) / Shadertoy (`mainImage`) `.frag` exports.
   - Wire `SpatialBrushKit` pointer velocity-vector drags, `Arrow` + `Space` / `Enter` reticle launches, and `.frag` shader exports into [`GalacticOrbitSimulator`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/astrophysics/galactic_orbits_tool.rs).
3. **Polyphonic Relativistic Redshift, Frame-Dragging, and Synchrotron Sonification (`math_explorer_gui/src/audio/`)**:
   - Add [`relativity_astrophysics.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/relativity_astrophysics.rs) mapping `clock_rate`, `FourVector::relativistic_doppler_factor`, `synchrotron_power`, and `inverse_compton_spectral_index` onto `20..=8000 Hz` stereo voices, rendered via `render_sonification_panel` (`Role::Meter` + `.wav` export) in both tabs.
   - Ramp silent horizon-guard frames (`active_voices == 0 && master_gain <= 1e-5`) and playback pauses with `MUTE_RAMP_TAU_SEC = 3 ms` in [`synth.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/synth.rs) and [`web_audio.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/web_audio.rs) so audio output decays below `-80 dBFS` within `30 ms` without clicks.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` users can sling relativistic photon fans and hypervelocity galactic tracers directly on the canvas, inspect exact Kerr frame-dragging clocks and Einstein rings, hear live gravitational/Doppler pitch shifts, and export `.png`, `.svg`, `.csv`, `.wav`, and `.frag` artifacts with zero heap allocations inside hot loops.
- **Positive**: All serialized tool states remain compact within `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4 KiB`), and optional crate boundaries compile cleanly under `--no-default-features --features "physics"`.
- **Neutral**: Full 4D off-equatorial Carter-constant volumetric ray marching and background worker protocol changes remain out of scope so every interactive kernel runs deterministically within a 16 ms frame budget on pre-allocated buffers.
