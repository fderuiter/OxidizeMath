# ADR 0041: Spatial Brush Kit and Accessible Direct Field Sculpting

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), [ADR 0027](0027-precompute-quantum-propagators-inplace-evolution.md), and [ADR 0032](0032-high-contrast-focus-selection-and-raster-tokens.md))

## Context
Although `InteractiveTool::on_brush(&mut self, ctx: &InteractionContext)` existed on the trait contract, zero workspace tools implemented it and `SimulationFramework` rendered custom tool canvases without populating brush stroke segments. Users exploring 2D Lattice Boltzmann aerodynamics, Turing reaction-diffusion morphogenesis, 1D Schrödinger wave-packet scattering, and 2D potential flows could only switch static scalar presets rather than directly sculpting obstacles, microfluidic channels, quantum potential landscapes, or vortex/source singularities on the live simulation canvas. Enabling direct spatial authoring across background-worker and main-thread solvers introduces four architectural requirements:
1. **Continuous Stroke Interpolation & Non-Blocking Worker Offload**: Rapid pointer drags skip grid cells unless interpolated via integer Bresenham traversal, and background `BackgroundWorker` threads ([ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md)) must accept `SimCommand::ApplyBrushStroke`, `SimCommand::ApplyBrush`, and `SimCommand::SyncSculptState` messages that emit fresh snapshots even when playback is paused.
2. **Zero-Allocation Solver Hot Loops**: Custom spatial masks, obstacles, and potential arrays must preserve the workspace's strict `0`-allocation `step()` invariant under `VerifiedAllocator`, without blowing up explicit finite-difference stability near sharp source/sink boundaries or recomputing $O(N^3)$ quantum matrix exponentials on every mouse-move event.
3. **WCAG 2.2 AA Keyboard Operability & Contrast**: Direct canvas painting must be fully operable without a pointer (`SC 2.1.1` Keyboard, `SC 2.1.2` No Keyboard Trap, `SC 1.4.11` Non-text Contrast `>= 3.0:1`, `SC 4.1.3` Status Messages) while preserving `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` focus traversal and global `Ctrl+Z` / `Cmd+Z` undo shortcuts.
4. **Compact URL Preset Persistence**: Arbitrary sculpted 2D grids and 1D curves must serialize through `save_state` / `try_load_state` ([ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md)) within `< 4 KiB` (`URL_JSON_BOUNDS`) shareable URL budgets.

## Decision
1. **`SpatialBrushKit` & Keyboard Cursor Reticle**:
   - Introduce [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/spatial_brush.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/spatial_brush.rs) providing pointer-to-grid mapping (`plot_to_grid_y_up` for bottom-up solver grids and `plot_to_grid_y_down` for top-down raster grids), continuous `BrushStroke` emission (`start`, `end`, `radius`, `mode`, `drag_vector`), and `InteractiveTool::on_brush` dispatch via `InteractionContext::from_brush_stroke`.
   - Expose a focusable plot canvas cursor with `Arrow` (`1x` step) / `Shift+Arrow` (`3x` coarse step) movement, `[` / `]` radius adjustment, `Space` / `Enter` stamping, non-trapping `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` navigation via arrow-only `EventFilter`, high-contrast focus borders and reticles via [`egui_plot/src/a11y.rs`](../../egui_plot/src/a11y.rs) `focus_ring_stroke`, and polite screen-reader announcements via `TwinSlotLiveRegion`.
2. **Async Worker Sculpt Commands & Domain Physics/Biology Kernels**:
   - Extend [`math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/state.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/async_sim/state.rs) with `SculptStatePayload`, `SimCommand::ApplyBrushStroke(BrushStroke)`, and `SimCommand::SyncSculptState(SculptStatePayload)`, coupled into `UnifiedSimTool`, `MorphogenesisRunner`, and `WaveRunner`.
   - Add NACA 4-digit cambered airfoil and Venturi nozzle stamping, live angle-of-attack $\alpha$ rotation, velocity-jet momentum injection, zero-gradient outflow, curl vorticity $\omega_z = \partial_x u_y - \partial_y u_x$, and bounce-back momentum-exchange $C_L$ / $C_D$ diagnostics in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/aerodynamics.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/aerodynamics.rs).
   - Add `ReactionCellRole`, `CellMask2D`, and positivity-preserving Patankar-diagonal `MaskedFiniteDifference2D` with homogeneous Neumann (`ZeroFluxWall`), `EmitterU`, `EmitterV`, `Sink`, and serpentine microfluidic maze stamping in [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/mask.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/mask.rs).
   - Add zero-allocation `construct_1d_hamiltonian_into`, `state_norm_squared`, and `transmission_probability` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/hamiltonian.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/hamiltonian.rs) and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/schrodinger.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/quantum/schrodinger.rs), and accelerate `time_evolution_operator` on finite real-symmetric Hamiltonians ($N \ge 4$) via orthogonal spectral decomposition $U(\Delta t) = Q \operatorname{diag}(e^{-i\lambda_m \Delta t/\hbar}) Q^T$ (falling back to `exp()` for general complex operators), updating `WaveRunner` Hamiltonians in $O(N)$ on brush edits while lazily refreshing the unitary propagator once per `step()` batch ([ADR 0027](0027-precompute-quantum-propagators-inplace-evolution.md)).
3. **Bounded RLE & Sparse Delta Preset Codec**:
   - Implement integer `bresenham_line` / `rasterize_disk_stroke`, bounded row-major `[value, run_length]` RLE encoding (`encode_rle_mask` / `decode_rle_mask` with `MAX_RLE_MASK_CELLS`), and quantized sparse `(index, value)` 1D potential delta encoding (`encode_sparse_potential` / `apply_sparse_potential`) in [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/brush_codec.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/brush_codec.rs), keeping serialized presets within `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4,096` bytes).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `LbmUnified`, `MorphogenesisTool`, `WaveSimulatorTool`, and `PotentialFlowTool` support interactive pointer and WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard sculpting, deterministic URL preset roundtrips under `4 KiB`, and `0` heap allocations in solver `step()` hot loops.
- **Neutral**: Unsculpted presets omit custom mask keys from `PresetState` and delegate directly to baseline unmasked kernels with zero runtime or URL overhead.
