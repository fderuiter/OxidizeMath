# ADR 0047: Zero-Allocation CSR Cotangent Laplace-Beltrami Operator, Thickness-Weighted Cortical Sheets, and 3D Riemannian PDE Studio

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0018](0018-uniform-spatial-grid-cortical-thickness.md), [ADR 0038](0038-watertight-3d-stl-and-ply-exports.md), [ADR 0040](0040-dual-backend-web-audio-sonification.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
[`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/) previously implemented `laplace_beltrami`, `HeatEquationSolver`, and `MeanCurvatureFlow` exclusively over 2D parameter charts $(u, v)$ whose metric determinant $\det(g) = EG - F^2 = R^4 \sin^2 v$ vanishes at polar coordinates ($v \in \{0, \pi\}$), while neither `HeatEquationSolver` nor `MeanCurvatureFlow` was exposed in `math_explorer_gui`. Simultaneously, [`crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/) exported eight cortical morphometry and GLM primitives (`Surface`, `bayesian_classification`, `internal_energy`, `external_energy`, `evolve_surface`, `cortical_thickness`, `estimate_beta`, `t_statistic`) with zero GUI consumers, allocated three heap `DVector<f64>` instances per vertex inside `internal_energy`, and rebuilt two `SpatialGrid` indexes on every single-vertex `cortical_thickness` call. Finally, [`TuringSystem`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/morphogenesis/mod.rs) (`SchnakenbergKinetics`, `GrayScottKinetics`) and [`HodgkinHuxleyNeuron`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/neuroscience/neuron.rs) were restricted to flat Euclidean grids or single-compartment ODEs without intrinsic 3D manifold diffusion or cortical-thickness modulation.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Allocation CSR Cotangent Laplace-Beltrami & Polar Singularity Guards (`pure_math`)**:
   - Add [`CotangentLaplacian`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/cotangent_mesh.rs) storing symmetric cotangent edge weights $w_{ij} = \frac{1}{2}(\cot \alpha_{ij} + \cot \beta_{ij})$ in Compressed Sparse Row (`row_offsets`, `col_indices`, `weights`) format paired with Meyer-Desbrun-Schröder-Barr (2003) mixed Voronoi vertex areas clamped to $\ge 10^{-12}$, `apply_at`, `apply`, `step_heat_euler`, and `compute_mean_curvature`.
   - Harden [`laplace_beltrami`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/operators.rs), [`HeatEquationSolver`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/heat_equation.rs), and [`DiscreteSurface::mean_curvature_vector`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/mean_curvature_flow.rs) with `det_g.abs() < 1e-12` and normal-norm degeneracy guards so polar vertices remain finite without `NaN`/`Inf` blow-ups.
2. **Zero-Allocation FreeSurfer Internal Energy & Batched Cortical Thickness (`domain_applied`)**:
   - Refactor [`internal_energy`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs) to accumulate first- and second-order discrete membrane/thin-plate differences on stack `[f64; 3]` arrays (`0` heap allocations under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`).
   - Add [`cortical_thickness_batch`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/thickness.rs) and `cortical_thickness_batch_into` constructing `(SpatialGrid::new(white), SpatialGrid::new(pial))` once per surface pair while preserving exact vertex-by-vertex numerical parity with `cortical_thickness`.
3. **Manifold Reaction-Diffusion & Thickness-Weighted Cortical Sheets (`domain_biology`)**:
   - Add [`CotangentMeshDiffusion`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/manifold.rs) implementing `DiffusionModel` and `SpatialDiffusion<N>` so `TuringSystem` evolves `SchnakenbergKinetics` and `GrayScottKinetics` directly on arbitrary triangulated 2-manifolds with zero hot-loop allocations.
   - Add [`CorticalSheetRunner`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/neuroscience/cortical_sheet.rs) coupling `HodgkinHuxleyNeuron` membrane vertices via symmetric `cortical_thickness`-weighted `CotangentLaplacian` lateral currents (`HodgkinHuxleyNeuron::set_v` added for zero-allocation geodesic stimulation).
4. **Interactive `RiemannianPdeStudio`, 3D Geodesic Brush, Sonification & Watertight 3D Exports (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Add opt-in [`SurfaceBrushController`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/surface_brush.rs) (`Orbit` vs. `Sculpt` mode via `Plot3DViewport::allow_primary_orbit`, front-facing barycentric triangle raycast picking, 4-hop CSR geodesic Gaussian kernel, and WCAG 2.2 AA `Arrow` + `Space`/`Enter` geodesic cursor with `EventFilter { tab: false, .. }` and `TwinSlotLiveRegion` announcements).
   - Register [`RiemannianPdeStudio`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/geometry_topology/riemannian_studio/mod.rs) (`<= 4,096` vertices, `< 4 KiB` `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` JSON state, four `StarterPresetMetadata` cards, cross-tab deep-links from `MorphogenesisTool` and `HodgkinHuxleyTool`, curvature-modulated `SonificationFrame` synthesis + `.wav` export, and watertight outward-wound `ColoredMesh` `.ply`, `.obj`, and `.stl` exports across closed and shell-extruded manifolds).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `CotangentLaplacian`, `CotangentMeshDiffusion`, `CorticalSheetRunner`, `MeanCurvatureFlow`, and `freesurfer::internal_energy` execute 100-step `VerifiedEngine::run_verified` loops with zero dynamic heap allocations and zero polar `NaN` singularities, while `cortical_thickness_batch` eliminates $O(V^2)$ redundant `SpatialGrid` allocations.
- **Positive**: All previously orphaned differential-geometry PDEs (`HeatEquationSolver`, `MeanCurvatureFlow`, `laplace_beltrami`) and all eight FreeSurfer morphometry/GLM exports are interactively inspectable, sonified, keyboard-accessible, and exportable to watertight per-vertex RGB `.ply`, `.obj`, and `.stl` 3D solids.
- **Neutral**: Multi-megabyte clinical NIfTI / `.mgz` neuroimaging volume uploads remain out of scope to preserve the `< 3 MiB` compressed WASM bundle budget and `< 4 KiB` stateless preset/URL bounds.
