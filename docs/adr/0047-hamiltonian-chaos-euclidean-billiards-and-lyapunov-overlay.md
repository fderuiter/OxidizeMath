# ADR 0047: Unified Hamiltonian Chaos Kernels, Flat Euclidean Dynamical Billiards, and Bifurcation Lyapunov Overlay

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0024](0024-deferred-modal-exports-and-plot-primitives.md), [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), and [ADR 0046](0046-symplectic-hamiltonian-solvers-and-poincare-lab.md))

## Context
Following [ADR 0046](0046-symplectic-hamiltonian-solvers-and-poincare-lab.md), `domain_physics::physics::chaos` provided continuous-time `HenonHeiles` and `DoublePendulum` flows, yet lacked discrete area-preserving symplectic maps (`ChirikovStandardMap`), a 2nd-order single-stage symplectic `ImplicitMidpoint` integrator for non-separable Hamiltonians, a unified `domain_physics::physics::chaos::hamiltonian` module with pendulum-fan and 3-body momentum perturbation helpers, and flat ($K = 0$) Euclidean dynamical billiards (`CircularBilliard`, `EllipticalBilliard`, `StadiumBilliard`, `SinaiBilliard`). On the GUI side, `ChaosTab` lacked an interactive Birkhoff phase-space workbench (`DynamicalBilliardsTool`), `HamiltonianChaosTool` needed discrete Chirikov torus seeding, `ImplicitMidpoint` selection, pendulum ensemble fans, figure-8 momentum perturbations, and non-destructive out-of-shell click notices, and `BifurcationDiagram` had not yet wired `logistic_lyapunov` or custom central-difference tangent sums into an interactive $\lambda(r)$ overlay. Finally, `papers/chaos_theory.tex` remained a brief placeholder unlinked to `chaos` in `traceability.toml`.

## Decision
1. **Single-Stage Symplectic `ImplicitMidpoint` Integrator (`pure_math`)**:
   - Extend [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/symplectic.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/symplectic.rs) with `SymplecticMethod::ImplicitMidpoint` and stack-backed `ImplicitMidpoint<State>` solving $y_{n+1} = y_n + h\,f(t_n + \tfrac{1}{2}h, \tfrac{1}{2}(y_n + y_{n+1}))$ via fixed-iteration stage updates and symmetric substepping with zero heap allocations.
2. **Unified `hamiltonian` and `billiards` Kernels (`domain_physics`)**:
   - Add [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/hamiltonian/`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/hamiltonian/mod.rs) consolidating `DoublePendulum`, `HenonHeiles`, `PoincareSection`, `FigureEightThreeBody`, area-preserving `ChirikovStandardMap` ($\det J \equiv 1$, Greene critical threshold $K_c \approx 0.971635$), `seed_double_pendulum_fan`, and center-of-mass-preserving `perturb_figure_eight_momentum`.
   - Add [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/billiards/`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/billiards/mod.rs) implementing exact analytical Euclidean ray-boundary intersections, specular reflections $\mathbf{v}' = \mathbf{v} - 2(\mathbf{v}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{n}})\hat{\mathbf{n}}$, unit-speed normalization, and Birkhoff phase-space coordinates $(s / L, \sin\phi) \in [0, 1) \times [-1, 1]$ across `CircularBilliard`, confocal `EllipticalBilliard` (conserving Berry's focal angular-momentum product $L_1 L_2$ with Ramanujan II + Gauss-Legendre arc-length quadrature), Bunimovich `StadiumBilliard`, and dispersing `SinaiBilliard`.
3. **Interactive `HamiltonianChaosTool`, `DynamicalBilliardsTool`, and `BifurcationDiagram` $\lambda(r)$ Overlay (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Upgrade [`HamiltonianChaosTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/hamiltonian.rs) (preserving `HamiltonianChaosLab` type alias and tool-selection compatibility) with `ChirikovStandardMap`, `ImplicitMidpoint`, 8-orbit pendulum fans, figure-8 momentum perturbation sliders, and inline notices rejecting clicks outside the energetically admissible shell without mutating active orbits.
   - Add [`DynamicalBilliardsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/billiards.rs) pairing AccessKit-instrumented spatial ray trails and ray fans with live 2D Birkhoff $(s/L, \sin\phi)$ phase portraits, 3 Starter Gallery presets, `<= 4 KiB` stateless URL serialization, and CSV/SVG/PNG exports.
   - Wire `logistic_lyapunov` and zero-allocation central-difference `CompiledExpr::eval_2d` tangent sums into [`BifurcationDiagram`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/chaos/bifurcation.rs) behind a toggleable $\lambda(r)$ overlay with a $\lambda = 0$ stability reference line.
4. **Scientific Traceability (`traceability.toml` and `papers/chaos_theory.tex`)**:
   - Bind `chaos = "chaos_theory.tex"` in [`traceability.toml`](../../traceability.toml), annotate `domain_physics::physics::chaos` and `ChaosTab` tools with `// [cite:chaos_theory]`, and expand [`papers/chaos_theory.tex`](../../papers/chaos_theory.tex) and [`papers/references.bib`](../../papers/references.bib) to cover dissipative maps, KAM theory, symplectic integrators, and Euclidean dynamical billiards.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Integrable circular and confocal elliptical billiards preserve unit speed to `< 1e-14` and focal product $L_1 L_2$ to `< 1e-12` over hundreds of bounces, contrasting invariant Birkhoff curves and whispering-gallery caustics directly against ergodic Bunimovich stadium and Sinai dispersing phase portraits with zero heap allocations in `#[verified]` kernels.
- **Positive**: `BifurcationDiagram` overlays $\lambda(r) < 0$ periodic windows and $\lambda(4) = \ln 2$ full chaos directly atop period-doubling cascades, while every Chaos tool traces to a compiled peer-cited LaTeX monograph.
- **Neutral**: Hyperbolic ($K = -1$) billiards, curved-spacetime geodesic billiards, Snell refraction, and scalar Helmholtz wave-billiard eigenmodes remain cleanly isolated in `pure_math::tensor` and `domain_physics::physics::optics`.
