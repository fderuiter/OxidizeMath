# ADR 20261123: `#![no_std]` Symbolic E-Graph Saturation and Hybrid Automatic Differentiation Engine

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Multiple mathematical engines and interactive laboratories in OxidizeMath previously approximated exact derivatives with ad-hoc central finite differences and duplicated domain ODE dynamics:

1. **Catastrophic Cancellation and Kink Spikes in Finite-Difference Stencils**:
   - Differential geometry in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs) used fixed `h = 1e-4` and `h = 1e-5` stencils, losing floating-point precision on shallow saddles (`z = 1e-4 * (x^2 - y^2)`), clamping `sanitize_surface_coord` to `[-50.0, 50.0]` inside the curvature kernel (`K ≈ -1941.5` boundary spikes), and invoking `36` primal metric calls per `laplace_beltrami` sample.
   - General-relativity Christoffel symbol computation in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs) relied on `h = 1e-5 * scale` central differences, degrading near black-hole event horizons (`r -> R_s`) and requiring loose `1e-4` tolerances.
   - Interactive AI and dynamical sandboxes (`ActivationFunctionsTool`, `LossLandscapeTool`, `ComplexMappingTool`, `bifurcation`, and nonlinear Levenberg-Marquardt curve fitting) evaluated numerical stencils (`1e-5` to `1e-6`), causing `~5e4` derivative spikes at piecewise activation thresholds (`ReLU`, `step`, `clamp`), `O(p)` forward passes per sample in curve fitting, and split Lyapunov derivative paths between synchronous and async bifurcation sweeps.
2. **Missing Symbolic Simplification and Exact Polynomial/AST Differentiation**:
   - [`crates/math_commons/src/ast`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/mod.rs) lacked symbolic `Expr::differentiate`, algebraic equality saturation, and reverse-mode gradient evaluation over pre-allocated `SolverWorkspace` buffers.
   - [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/algebra/polynomial.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/algebra/polynomial.rs) lacked exact `Polynomial::differentiate`.
3. **Duplicated Hand-Derived EKF ODEs and Jacobians in GUI**:
   - [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/closed_loop_control/ekf.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/ai/closed_loop_control/ekf.rs) maintained hand-coded `lorenz_rhs_and_jac`, `seir_rhs_and_jac`, and `pk_rhs_and_jac` functions alongside a bespoke `rk4_step_with_jacobian` integrator, duplicating canonical domain dynamics in `domain_physics`, `domain_epidemiology`, and `domain_applied`.

## Decision
1. **Create `#![no_std]` Engine Crate `crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic`**:
   - Introduce [`crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic/src/lib.rs) (`#![no_std]`, optional `alloc`, WASM-compatible) with zero-allocation forward-mode scalars [`Dual<T>`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic/src/dual.rs) (`f64` and `Complex<f64>`) and [`Jet2<N>`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic/src/jet2.rs) (exact primal, gradient `[f64; N]`, and symmetric Hessian `[[f64; N]; N]`), implementing deterministic Clarke subgradients at non-smooth kinks (`abs`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `step`, `rect`).
   - Implement [`EGraph`](../../crates/02_engines/engine_symbolic/src/egraph.rs) equality saturation with hard safety caps (`MAX_EGRAPH_NODES = 2048`, `MAX_REWRITE_PASSES = 16`), union-find congruency closure, algebraic/trigonometric/hyperbolic/logarithmic rewrites, and strictly iterative post-order AST cost extraction without recursion.
   - Provide [`DualArrayState<N>`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/ode/dual_state.rs), `ode_rhs_dual_jacobian`, and `rk4_step_dual_jacobian` in `pure_math::analysis::ode` to lift `OdeSystem<DualArrayState<N>>` implementations through RK4 in `N` stack-only `Dual<f64>` evaluations.
2. **Extend `math_commons` and `pure_math` with Symbolic Differentiation and Reverse-Mode Tape Evaluation**:
   - Add `Polynomial::differentiate` in [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/algebra/polynomial.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/algebra/polynomial.rs) and `Expr::differentiate` and `Expr::simplify` (emitting simplified LaTeX and `to_glsl_scalar` / `to_glsl_complex` shaders) in [`crates/math_commons/src/ast/symbolic.rs`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/symbolic.rs).
   - Lift [`CompiledExpr`](../../crates/math_commons/src/ast/compiled_autodiff.rs) generically over `EvalScalar` (`eval_1d_dual`, `eval_dir_dual`, `eval_complex_z_params_dual`, `eval_2d_jet2`, `eval_3d_jet2`) and add reverse-mode `eval_grad_into` backed by pre-allocated `SolverWorkspace` buffers (`0` heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`).
   - Migrate [`levenberg_marquardt_numeric`](../../crates/math_commons/src/fit/lm.rs), `egui_plot`, and `fit_bridge` so custom AST residual Jacobians execute in `1` reverse pass per data point instead of `2p + 1` finite-difference evaluations.
3. **Migrate Differential Geometry, General Relativity, Domain ODEs, and GUI Sandboxes**:
   - Upgrade [`ParametricSurface`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/differential_geometry/surface.rs) (`position_jet2`, `laplace_beltrami` in `1` `Jet2<2>` pass), [`christoffel_symbols`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/tensor/christoffel.rs) (plus `Metric::metric_dual_at` on Schwarzschild, Kerr, and Morris-Thorne metrics in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/high_energy/general_relativity/schwarzschild.rs)), and vector calculus operators (`gradient_dual`, `divergence_dual`, `curl_dual`, `laplacian_jet2`). Move `[-50.0, 50.0]` coordinate clamping out of `sanitize_surface_coord` into viewport/STL mesh projection via `clamp_surface_point`.
   - Implement `#[verified]` `OdeSystem<DualArrayState<N>>` on `LorenzSystem`, `SEIRDynamics`/`SEIRModel`, and `BatemanCompartmentDynamics`, add [`DualKalmanModel`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/algorithms/kalman_dual.rs) in `domain_applied`, and delete `lorenz_rhs_and_jac`, `seir_rhs_and_jac`, and `pk_rhs_and_jac` from `ClosedLoopControlTool`.
   - Migrate `ActivationFunctionsTool`, `LossLandscapeTool`, `ComplexMappingTool`, and unified sync/async `bifurcation` Lyapunov sweeps to exact forward/reverse AD, leaving discrete voxel differencing in `crates/domain_applied/src/applied/freesurfer/surface.rs` untouched.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Shallow-saddle Gaussian curvature (`-4.0e-8`) resolves within `1e-20`, near-horizon Schwarzschild Christoffel symbols (`r = 1.00002`) match analytical values within `1e-11` relative error, `laplace_beltrami` drops from `36` primal evaluations to `1` `Jet2<2>` pass, nonlinear AST curve fitting drops from `2p + 1` evaluations to `1` reverse-mode pass per sample, Clarke subgradients eliminate piecewise activation spikes, and `ClosedLoopControlTool` reuses verified domain ODEs directly.
- **Negative / Trade-offs**: `EGraph` saturation (`alloc` feature) allocates bounded arena vectors during compile-time / UI expression editing (`<= 2048` nodes, `<= 16` passes), while all simulation-loop hot paths (`Dual`, `Jet2`, `eval_grad_into`, `rk4_step_dual_jacobian`) remain strictly zero-allocation under `VerifiedAllocator`.
