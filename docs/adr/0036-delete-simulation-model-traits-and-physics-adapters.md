# ADR 0036: Delete Dead `SimulationModel` Traits and Physics Adapters

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0016](0016-delete-unreferenced-boundary-conditions.md), [ADR 0019](0019-purge-dead-code-bypasses.md), [ADR 0027](0027-delete-dead-grid2d-and-triangle-types.md), and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
Active simulations across `pure_math`, `domain_*`, `math_explorer`, and `math_explorer_gui` step solvers through `TimeStepper`, `EvolutionEngine`, and `SimulationFramework`. Following the MRI framework migration onto `SimulationFramework` and the curated `math_explorer` facade re-exports in [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md), `crates/oxidize_core` and `crates/domain_physics` still retained obsolete lifecycle traits, unused adapter structs, and unread solver configuration fields:
1. **`oxidize_core` Lifecycle Traits and Stale Documentation**: [`crates/oxidize_core/src/lib.rs`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/lib.rs) exported `ModelConfig`, `ModelState`, `SimulationModel`, and `pub mod prelude` with 7 `#[allow(missing_docs)]` attributes alongside a `BooleanMaskSimulation` unit test that pulled an otherwise unused `thiserror` dependency in [`crates/oxidize_core/Cargo.toml`](../../crates/oxidize_core/Cargo.toml). [`crates/oxidize_core/README.md`](../../crates/oxidize_core/README.md) inaccurately claimed that all six domain crates implemented `oxidize_core` lifecycle traits.
2. **Uncalled `domain_physics` Adapters and Unread Solver Fields**:
   - [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/lorenz.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/lorenz.rs) defined unreferenced `LorenzConfig` and `impl SimulationModel for LorenzSystem` (6 `#[allow(missing_docs)]` attributes) plus `dt` and `integration_method` fields and builder setters on `LorenzBuilder` and `LorenzSystem` that were never read by `LorenzSystem::step`, `LorenzSystem::step_with`, `OdeSystem`, or `TimeStepper`.
   - [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs) defined unreferenced `LbmConfig` and `impl SimulationModel for LatticeBoltzmannD2Q9<BgkCollision>` (4 `#[allow(missing_docs)]` attributes and `#[verified_engine::verified(allow_recursion = ...)]`) alongside the active `EvolutionEngine` implementation.
   - [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/bloch.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/bloch.rs) defined unreferenced `BlochConfig` (with unread `b0`), `BlochState`, and `impl SimulationModel for BlochSimulator` (11 `#[allow(missing_docs)]` attributes) plus unread `dt` and `integration_method` fields on `BlochSimulator`.
   - [`crates/domain_physics/Cargo.toml`](../../crates/domain_physics/Cargo.toml) declared `serde` solely for these dead config structs, and [`math_explorer/tests/facade_macros.rs`](../../math_explorer/tests/facade_macros.rs) was the lone non-adapter caller, invoking `SimulationModel` only to verify `VerifiedEngine` metric recording.

## Decision
1. **Delete `SimulationModel` Lifecycle Traits from `oxidize_core`**: Remove `ModelConfig`, `ModelState`, `SimulationModel`, `BooleanMaskSimulation` tests, and `pub mod prelude` from [`crates/oxidize_core/src/lib.rs`](../../crates/oxidize_core/src/lib.rs), and drop the unused `thiserror` dependency from [`crates/oxidize_core/Cargo.toml`](../../crates/oxidize_core/Cargo.toml) while retaining `serde` for `TraceabilityReport`.
2. **Align `oxidize_core` Documentation**: Rewrite [`crates/oxidize_core/README.md`](../../crates/oxidize_core/README.md) to document all nine active modules (`ast_visitor`, `boundary`, `iteration`, `mesh`, `path_utils`, `rng`, `traceability`, `vfs`, and `workspace`) and accurately describe ecosystem integration with `TimeStepper`, `EvolutionEngine`, and `SimulationFramework`.
3. **Delete Dead Physics Adapters and Unread Solver Fields**:
   - Remove `LorenzConfig`, `SimulationModel`, and `dt` / `integration_method` fields and setters from `LorenzBuilder` and `LorenzSystem` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/lorenz.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/chaos/lorenz.rs).
   - Remove `LbmConfig`, `ModelConfig`, `ModelState`, and `SimulationModel` (including `allow_recursion`) from [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs).
   - Remove `BlochConfig`, `BlochState`, `SimulationModel`, and unread `dt` / `integration_method` fields on `BlochSimulator` from [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/bloch.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/mri/bloch.rs).
   - Remove unused `serde` from [`crates/domain_physics/Cargo.toml`](../../crates/domain_physics/Cargo.toml) while retaining `oxidize_core` for `OxidizeRng`.
4. **Update `math_explorer` Macro Verification Test & API Baseline**: Update `test_migrated_physics_methods_record_verified_metrics` in [`math_explorer/tests/facade_macros.rs`](../../math_explorer/tests/facade_macros.rs) to exercise `LorenzSystem::step`, `LatticeBoltzmannD2Q9::new`, and `LatticeBoltzmann::step` directly without `oxidize_core` imports, and regenerate [`crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json`](../../crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json) to prune the 19 deleted public signatures.

## Consequences
- **Positive**:
  - Eliminates dead `SimulationModel` traits, four dead physics config/state structs, unread solver fields (`dt`, `integration_method`, `b0`), 28 `#[allow(missing_docs)]` attributes, one `verified(allow_recursion)` attribute, and two unused crate dependencies (`thiserror` in `oxidize_core` and `serde` in `domain_physics`).
  - Prunes 19 obsolete signatures from `crates/unified_verification/api_baseline.json` and aligns `crates/oxidize_core/README.md` with active runtime modules.
- **Negative**:
  - External callers referencing `oxidize_core::{ModelConfig, ModelState, SimulationModel, prelude}`, `LorenzConfig`, `LbmConfig`, `BlochConfig`, `BlochState`, or `LorenzBuilder::{dt, integration_method}` must use `TimeStepper` or `EvolutionEngine` directly, matching all workspace consumers.
