# ADR 0038: `WorkspaceDataBus` Cross-Domain Graph Coupling and Zero-Allocation Graph Solvers

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0009](0009-reusable-graph-layout-algorithms.md), [ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), and [ADR 0034](0034-curate-math-explorer-facade-exports-and-doctests.md))

## Context
[`MathExplorerApp`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/mod.rs) supports multi-pane workspaces (`Single`, `SplitHorizontal`, `SplitVertical`, and `QuadGrid`), yet individual panes previously operated as isolated state islands:
1. **No Typed Inter-Pane Topology Channel**: Sculpting a signed weighted network in [`GraphEditorTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/graph_editor.rs) could not propagate live to graph metrics, pathfinding visualizers, epidemic contact networks, Hodgkin-Huxley neural circuits, spin glasses, or spatial evolutionary games without breaking the signature stability of [`ExplorerTab`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/mod.rs) (`show_inside(&mut self, ui: &mut egui::Ui, frame: &mut eframe::Frame)`) or [`InteractiveTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/mod.rs) (`show_inside(&mut self, ui: &mut egui::Ui)`). Meanwhile, `federated_registry` in `diagnostics` is strictly scoped to diagnostic telemetry rather than scientific simulation state.
2. **Lattice-Only Spin and Mean-Field-Only Replicator Solvers**: [`SpinLattice`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/ising.rs) models regular 2D periodic grids with uniform coupling $J$, preventing geometric frustration experiments on arbitrary signed graphs ($J_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}$, frustrated odd cycles). Similarly, `replicator_step` in `math_explorer::applied::game_theory::evolutionary` models well-mixed ODE populations, lacking spatial neighborhood payoff accumulation where cooperator clusters survive temptation $T > 1$.
3. **Safety and Allocation Bounds Under Live Sculpting**: Continuous brush strokes in `GraphEditorTool` can rapidly grow node counts beyond safe interactive budgets for stiff ODEs (`NeuralNetwork`) or per-frame canvas rendering, and untrusted `.mws` session files or `#state=` share URLs could inject unbounded graph payloads or unknown fields unless strictly validated.

## Decision
1. **Typed `WorkspaceDataBus` Projected onto `egui::Context`**:
   - Introduce [`WorkspaceDataBus`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/data_bus/mod.rs) on `MathExplorerApp` storing revisioned `Option<Arc<SharedGraph>>` (`nodes: Vec<SharedNode>`, signed `edges: Vec<(usize, usize, f64)>`) and `Option<Arc<EpidemicTraceSignal>>` (`population`, `beta`, `gamma`, `heterogeneous_r0`, `susceptible`, `infected`, `recovered`).
   - Project `Arc` handles into `egui::Context::data_mut` before rendering panes across `Single`, `SplitHorizontal`, `SplitVertical`, and `QuadGrid` layouts, and harvest monotonic revision updates back onto `MathExplorerApp::data_bus` after pane rendering (requesting a single follow-up repaint when a downstream pane publishes a new revision so earlier panes converge deterministically without altering `ExplorerTab` or `InteractiveTool` trait signatures).
   - Provide per-pane `Follow Workspace Graph` opt-out toggles in multi-pane headers ([`pane_chrome.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/app/viewport/pane_chrome.rs)) and tool controls with polite `AccessKit` live announcements and WCAG AA contrast tokens, allowing side-by-side comparison of frozen vs. live topologies.
2. **Tactile Signed-Edge Sculpting and Six Coupled Cross-Domain Consumers**:
   - Upgrade [`GraphEditorTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/graph_editor.rs) with excitatory (`+w`) and inhibitory (`-w`) edge-painting brushes, a $K_{1,k}$ hub-and-spoke stamp, a drag-across edge-severing stroke, and normalized `[0, 1]^2` `SharedGraph` publication on every graph mutation, undo/redo, layout, or preset load.
   - Couple six tools across five scientific domains with safe node budgets and polite warning banners:
     - [`NetworkMetricsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/network_metrics.rs) and [`AlgorithmVisualizerTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/graph_theory/algorithm_visualizer.rs) (live degree/clustering/component/diameter invariants and BFS/DFS/Dijkstra re-runs).
     - [`NetworkPropagationTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/epidemiology/network_propagation.rs) (`<= 500` nodes, computing analytical `heterogeneous_r0` and streaming `EpidemicTraceSignal`) paired with empirical stochastic $S(t), I(t), R(t)$ overlays in [`SirTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/epidemiology/sir.rs).
     - [`NeuralNetworkVizTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/neuroscience/neural_network_viz.rs) (`<= 32` Hodgkin-Huxley neurons, mapping positive/negative edge weights to excitatory/inhibitory `Synapse` conductances).
     - [`IsingModelTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/solid_state/ising.rs) (`<= 1000` spins, switching to `GraphSpinSystem` when following a live graph while preserving 2D `SpinLattice` mode).
     - [`ReplicatorDynamicsTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/game_theory/replicator.rs) (`<= 500` agents, overlaying spatial graph trajectories alongside the mean-field ODE curve).
   - Register four cross-domain `StarterPresetMetadata` cards (`hub_spoke_superspreaders`, `ei_neural_rings`, `frustrated_triangular_spins`, and `spatial_cooperators`) that seed initial `SharedGraph` topologies when the workspace bus is empty.
3. **Zero-Allocation CSR Domain Solvers and Bounded Persistence**:
   - Add [`GraphSpinSystem`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/stat_mech/graph_ising.rs) (`math_explorer::physics::stat_mech::GraphSpinSystem`) and [`GraphReplicatorSystem`](../../crates/domain_applied/src/applied/game_theory/evolutionary/graph_replicator.rs) (`math_explorer::applied::game_theory::evolutionary::GraphReplicatorSystem`) backed by compressed sparse row (CSR) adjacency arrays and pre-allocated scratch buffers so `evolve`, `metropolis_step`, `frustrated_edge_count`, and `step` execute with zero heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator` and `OxidizeRng`.
   - Enforce `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]`, finite-coordinate/weight checks, and [`ToolStateBounds`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/state_bounds.rs) / [`URL_JSON_BOUNDS`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/url_state/codec.rs) caps on serialized `WorkspaceDataBus` payloads in `.mws` workspace sessions and `#state=` share URLs.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can sculpt signed networks once in `GraphEditorTool` and immediately observe structural invariants, heterogeneous epidemic thresholds, excitatory-inhibitory spiking dynamics, geometric spin frustration, and spatial cooperation clusters across split or quad panes.
- **Positive**: `SpinLattice`, `ExplorerTab`, `InteractiveTool`, and `federated_registry` contracts remain completely unchanged, while `GraphSpinSystem` and `GraphReplicatorSystem` maintain strict zero-allocation hot-loop guarantees verified by `VerifiedAllocator`.
- **Neutral**: Real-time Web Audio sonification remains out of scope, and oversized topologies above per-domain interactive budgets (`32` Hodgkin-Huxley neurons, `500` epidemic/game nodes, `1000` spins) deterministically clamp to induced leading subgraphs with accessible warning banners.
