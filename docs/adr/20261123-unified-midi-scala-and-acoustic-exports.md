# ADR 20261123: Unified SMF Format 1 MIDI, Huygens-Fokker Scala, and Acoustic Impulse-Response Exports

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0038](0038-prime-exponent-fractran-vm-and-minsky-gui.md), [ADR 0040](0040-dual-backend-web-audio-sonification.md), and [ADR 0042](0042-2d-chladni-plate-and-wave-tank-solver.md))

## Context
[ADR 0040](0040-dual-backend-web-audio-sonification.md) introduced stack-allocated [`SonificationFrame`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/frame.rs) synthesis and `-3.0 dBFS` safety limiting across seven simulation tools, while [ADR 0038](0038-prime-exponent-fractran-vm-and-minsky-gui.md) and [ADR 0042](0042-2d-chladni-plate-and-wave-tank-solver.md) added prime-exponent FRACTRAN counter machines and zero-allocation 2D Kirchhoff-Love Chladni plate / wave-tank solvers. However, three architectural gaps prevented composers, sound designers, and educators from exporting studio-grade musical and acoustic artifacts:
1. **UI-Buffered 3-Second `.wav` Ceiling**: `AudioController` previously recorded a `180`-frame (`3.0 s` at `60 Hz`) UI ring buffer, tying `.wav` exports to live frame pacing rather than deterministic multi-second (`1 s` to `15 s` at `44.1 kHz` or `48 kHz`) offline synthesis.
2. **Missing Zero-Dependency Microtonal MIDI & Scala Encoders**: Shared export queues ([`export_queue.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_queue.rs)) and browser MIME inference ([`file_io.rs`](../../egui_plot/src/file_io.rs)) lacked `.mid` (`audio/midi`), `.wav` (`audio/wav`), `.scl` (`text/plain`), and `.kbm` (`text/plain`) support, and no zero-dependency serializers existed to translate prime harmonic ratios or PDE modal spectra into DAW-ready SMF Format 1 scores or Surge XT / Vital tuning tables.
3. **Silent 2D Chladni Plates and FRACTRAN Register Machines**: [`ChladniWaveTankTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/chladni_wave_tank.rs) lacked convolution-reverb `.wav` impulse-response capture from draggable stereo pickup transducers, while [`FractranMachineTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/number_theory/fractran_machine/mod.rs) and [`FactorizationTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/number_theory/factorization.rs) lacked unified `.wav`, `.mid`, `.scl`, and `.kbm` export dispatch.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Dependency SMF Format 1 MIDI and Huygens-Fokker Scala Serializers**:
   - Implement [`midi.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/midi.rs) in pure `std` Rust (`480` ticks/quarter note, `120 BPM` conductor `MTrk`, non-percussion channels `0..8` skipping GM channel `9`, variable-length delta quantities, and RPN `00 00` `±2`-semitone 14-bit pitch bends achieving `0.0244` cents/LSB resolution—well within `<= 0.5` cents for prime harmonics).
   - Implement [`scala.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/scala.rs) and [`just_intonation_rational`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/number_theory.rs) to serialize exact octave-reduced rationals (`num/den` without `.`) and inharmonic modal intervals (`cents` with `.`) into Huygens-Fokker `.scl` scales and `.kbm` root-key mappings compatible with Surge XT and Vital.
2. **Deterministic `1 s`–`15 s` Offline `.wav` Synthesis & Shared Framework Export Pipeline**:
   - Extend [`wav.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/wav.rs), [`audio/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mod.rs), and [`audio/ui.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/ui.rs) with [`OfflineRenderConfig`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/wav.rs) (`1.0..=15.0 s`, `44.1 kHz` or `48 kHz`, bounded to `MAX_OFFLINE_STEREO_FRAMES = 720,000`) and zero-allocation `render_offline_pcm_into` over [`OfflinePcmSynthesizer`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/audio/synth.rs).
   - Extend [`ExportCapabilities`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_types.rs), [`ExportFormat`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_queue.rs), [`export_audio.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/export_audio.rs), and [`infer_mime_type`](../../egui_plot/src/file_io.rs) to dispatch `.wav`, `.mid`, `.scl`, and `.kbm` across existing sonified tools (`HodgkinHuxleyTool`, `SpikeAnalysisTool`, `NeuralNetworkViz`, `WaveSim`, `AttractorPlotter`, `BifurcationDiagram`) and microtonal studio tools.
3. **Stereo Chladni Impulse Responses and Number-Theory Polyphony**:
   - Add [`chladni_wave_tank/acoustics.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/chladni_wave_tank/acoustics.rs) with draggable `L`/`R` stereo pickups on [`canvas.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/analysis/chladni_wave_tank/canvas.rs) and pre-allocated [`Wave2DSolver`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/wave_2d.rs) impulse-response capture normalized within `-3.0 dBFS` and matching Leissa clamped-square analytical eigenfrequencies ([`analytic_clamped_square_omega`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/wave_2d/config.rs)) within `2%`.
   - Wire zero-allocation register-stepped PCM synthesis (`render_fractran_pcm_into` in [`fractran_machine/acoustics.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/number_theory/fractran_machine/acoustics.rs)) plus `.mid`, `.scl`, and `.kbm` exports into [`FractranMachineTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/number_theory/fractran_machine/mod.rs) and [`FactorizationTool`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/number_theory/factorization.rs).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can download DAW-ready microtonal `.mid` scores, Surge XT / Vital `.scl` + `.kbm` tuning pairs, deterministic `1 s`–`15 s` (`44.1`/`48 kHz`) `.wav` recordings, and stereo Chladni convolution-reverb impulse responses directly in the browser with zero new Cargo dependencies and `< 3 MB` compressed WASM bundles.
- **Positive**: Inner PCM synthesis, 2D Verlet impulse stepping, and FRACTRAN register loops remain zero-allocation under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified`, maintain `-3.0 dBFS` limiter ceilings and explicit user-gesture unmuting under `'self'` CSP, and emit polite WCAG 2.2 AA announcements.
- **Neutral**: Synchronous offline `.wav` and `.mid` rendering is bounded at `15.0 s` (`720,000` stereo frames) to guarantee responsive main-thread downloads in WebAssembly without `AudioWorklet` scripts.
