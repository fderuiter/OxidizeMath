# ADR 0046: Co-Stepped Advective Morphogenesis Studio and Zero-Allocation LBM Velocity Coupling

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0012](0012-background-simulation-worker-offload.md), [ADR 0022](0022-unify-preset-state-serialization.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
Previously, `LatticeBoltzmannD2Q9` fluid aerodynamics (`domain_physics`) and `TuringSystem` reaction-diffusion morphogenesis (`domain_biology`) evolved in completely disconnected solvers: `StencilOperators` only implemented 1D horizontal `upwind_flux`, `TuringSystem` lacked spatial velocity fields and zero-flux obstacle coupling, and reading fluid velocities out of `LatticeState` required per-cell queries across column-major `nalgebra::DMatrix` storage. Coupling live Kármán vortex wakes with `SchnakenbergKinetics` and `GrayScottKinetics` morphogens introduces four architectural requirements:
1. **Conservative Zero-Flux 2D Upwind Advection**: Spatially varying incompressible and weakly compressible Lattice Boltzmann wakes require 2D upwind advection that preserves mass conservation (`< 1e-11`) at domain borders and internal solid obstacle walls while reducing identically to pure central-difference diffusion when fluid velocity vanishes.
2. **Zero-Allocation Transpose Coupling**: `LatticeState` stores macroscopic velocity matrices (`ux`, `uy`) and solid masks (`obstacles`) in column-major `nalgebra::DMatrix` (`(y, x)`) layout, whereas `TuringSystem` and `StencilOperators` operate on flat row-major slices (`flatten_2d_index(x, y, width)`). Bridging the two every frame must execute with `0` heap allocations under `VerifiedAllocator`.
3. **Combined Diffusive-Advective CFL Stability**: Co-stepping BGK fluid relaxation (`tau = 3 * nu + 0.5`) alongside stiff cubic autocatalysis (`Schnakenberg`, `Gray-Scott`) requires enforcing a combined diffusive + advective Courant-Friedrichs-Lewy step ceiling $\Delta t \le \alpha / (2 D_{\max}(h_x^{-2} + h_y^{-2}) + |\mathrm{Pe}|(|u_x|_{\max} h_x^{-1} + |u_y|_{\max} h_y^{-1}))$ and `FiniteStateGuard` recovery.
4. **Compact URL Sculpting & Accessible Cross-Domain Workflow**: Sculpted `100 x 50` (`5,000`-cell) wind-tunnel obstacle masks must round-trip under `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` (`4,096` bytes), support pointer and WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard sculpting without trapping `Tab`, and expose a direct cross-domain launch shortcut from `FluidDynamicsTab` into `MorphogenesisTab`.

## Decision
1. **2D Upwind & Masked Zero-Flux Stencil Operators (`pure_math`)**:
   - Extend [`crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/fused_stepper.rs`](../../crates/pure_math/src/pure_math/analysis/pde/fused_stepper.rs) with precomputed `inv_dy`, `inv_two_dy`, `StencilNeighbors2D`, `upwind_flux_y`, `upwind_flux_2d`, conservative finite-volume `upwind_divergence_2d`, and `FusedStencilStepper::step_2d_coupled_masked_neumann`, zeroing normal face velocities and reflecting neighbor concentrations across solid obstacle boundaries.
2. **Zero-Allocation Row-Major LBM Velocity & Obstacle Copier (`domain_physics`)**:
   - Add `copy_velocity_row_major`, `copy_obstacles_row_major`, and `copy_velocity_and_obstacles_row_major` on `LatticeState<Q>` and `LatticeBoltzmann<Q, L, C>` in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/state.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/state.rs) and [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs`](../../crates/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/lattice_boltzmann/algorithms.rs), writing column-major `(y, x)` fields directly into preallocated row-major slices (`flatten_2d_index(x, y, width)`).
3. **Advective 2D Reaction-Diffusion Solver (`domain_biology`)**:
   - Introduce `AdvectiveFiniteDifference2D` in [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/advective.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/diffusion/fd2d/advective.rs) and `TuringSystem::new_advective_2d` (with `velocity_and_obstacles_mut`, `set_velocity_and_obstacles`, `pin_solid_species`, and `max_cfl_dt`) in [`crates/domain_biology/src/biology/morphogenesis/advective.rs`](../../crates/domain_biology/src/biology/morphogenesis/advective.rs).
4. **`AdvectiveMorphogenesisTool` Studio & Compact Hex Mask Sharing (`math_explorer_gui`)**:
   - Extract shared `100 x 50` fairing stamps and `1,250`-char hexadecimal bitpack codecs (`encode_mask_hex` / `decode_mask_hex`) into [`math_explorer_gui/src/framework/fairing_mask.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/framework/fairing_mask.rs), keeping sculpted URL payloads well below `4,096` bytes.
   - Implement `AdvectiveMorphogenesisTool` in [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/advective/mod.rs`](../../math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/morphogenesis/advective/mod.rs) with `SchnakenbergSpots`, `SchnakenbergStripes`, `GrayScottMitosis`, and `GrayScottCoral` presets, live bounce-back `F_D` / `F_L` / `C_D` readout, pointer + keyboard obstacle sculpting, `.csv` wake-plume and `.png`/`.gif` raster exports, and a direct launch shortcut from `FluidDynamicsTab`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Users can sculpt aerodynamic obstacles and watch live Kármán vortex wakes shear Turing spots and Gray-Scott coral plumes at interactive frame rates with `0` heap allocations per co-stepped tick across both `SchnakenbergKinetics` and `GrayScottKinetics`.
- **Neutral**: Baseline pure-diffusion `FiniteDifference2D` solvers remain unchanged, and `AdvectiveFiniteDifference2D` at `peclet = 0.0` with empty obstacle masks matches `FiniteDifference2D` bit-for-bit.
