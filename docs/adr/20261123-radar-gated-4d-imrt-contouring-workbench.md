# ADR 20261123: Live 4D Radar-Gated IMRT Contouring Workbench, Zero-Allocation Link Bus, and Medical Sonification

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0016](0016-formal-beam-state-machine-guards.md), [ADR 0037](0037-consolidate-medical-physics-kernels.md), [ADR 0040](0040-dual-backend-web-audio-sonification.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
[`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/radar_gating/mod.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/radar_gating/mod.rs) previously discarded the verified [`BeamState`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/radar_gating/gating.rs) output across six simulation and UI call sites, leaving [`DoseCalculationTool`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/dose/mod.rs) disconnected on a static circular phantom. Meanwhile, verified radiotherapy kernels in [`crates/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/medical/mod.rs) (`evaluation::pearson_correlation`, `evaluation::root_mean_square_error`, `evaluation::spatial_accuracy_error`, `accelerator::beam_loading_energy`, and `optical_motion::validation::dice_similarity_coefficient`), [`SpatialBrushKit`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/framework/spatial_brush.rs) RLE field sculpting, and polyphonic sonification in [`math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/mod.rs) had zero medical workbench consumers, and both `dose.rs` (`483` lines) and `imrt.rs` (`485` lines) sat near the 500-line file ceiling.

## Decision
1. **Zero-Allocation Thread-Local `GatingTelemetry` Link Bus and Single-Pane 4D Fallback**:
   - Add [`math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/link_bus.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/link_bus.rs) (`<= 140` lines) storing `Copy` `GatingTelemetry` snapshots inside a fixed `const { Cell::new(LinkBusRing::new()) }` ring (`GATING_RING_CAPACITY = 64`) with monotonic sequence cursors and zero heap allocations.
   - Wire all six `step_gating()` call sites plus lifecycle controls in [`radar_gating/mod.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/radar_gating/mod.rs) and [`radar_gating/ui.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/radar_gating/ui.rs) to publish `GatingTelemetry` containing verified `pearson_correlation`, `root_mean_square_error`, and `spatial_accuracy_error` computed over preallocated `HISTORY_LEN` displacement rings.
   - Embed a zero-allocation `Standalone4dStepper` in [`imrt/motion.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/imrt/motion.rs) so single-pane `DoseCalculationTool` users can run gated delivery, inject `+10 mm` coughs, and test `SafetyInterlock` without opening two panes, while `SplitHorizontal` multi-pane layouts automatically consume live `RadarGatingTool` telemetry from `link_bus`.
2. **Modular `imrt/` and `dose/` Subsystems with 4D Tissue Shift, `SpatialBrushKit` RLE Contours, and Gantry Handles**:
   - Split `imrt.rs` into [`imrt/mod.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/imrt/mod.rs), [`imrt/motion.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/imrt/motion.rs), [`imrt/contours.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/imrt/contours.rs), and [`imrt/state.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/imrt/state.rs), and split `dose.rs` into [`dose/mod.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/dose/mod.rs), [`dose/ui.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/dose/ui.rs), [`dose/canvas.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/dose/canvas.rs), and [`dose/tests.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/medical/dose/tests.rs).
   - Scale active 4D beam fluence by `accelerator::beam_loading_energy(beam_current_ma) / beam_loading_energy(0.0)`, bilinearly sample tissue-shifted room dose into parallel preallocated `gated_dose_vec` (`BeamActive` only) and `ungated_dose_vec` (halted only on `Standby` / `SafetyInterlock`), and compute `dice_similarity_coefficient` (`95% D_Rx` isodose vs. PTV mask) in place.
   - Extend [`BrushMode`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/framework/spatial_brush.rs) with `PtvTarget` and `OrganAtRisk`, persist sculpted contours via `encode_rle_mask` / `decode_rle_mask` on `DoseState` within `< 4 KiB` `URL_JSON_BOUNDS` while preserving byte-for-byte default JSON compatibility (`{"amplitude":1.0,"beta":1.0,"width":200,"height":200}`), and render four draggable + keyboard-nudgeable coplanar gantry handles with `announce_live_status` ARIA feedback and non-trapping `Tab` focus.
3. **Polyphonic Medical Sonification and `<= 30 ms` Interlock Suspension**:
   - Add [`math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/medical.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/medical.rs) (`<= 180` lines) exporting feature-independent `LinacGatePhase`, `radar_linac_frame`, and `imrt_delivery_frame` alongside [`math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/medical_radar.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/audio/mappings/medical_radar.rs) (`medical_radar_frame` in `RadarGatingTool::sonification_frame`), render `render_sonification_panel` (with AccessKit `Role::Meter` and `.wav` export) in both medical tools, and invoke `notify_solver_diverged()` on `BeamState::SafetyInterlock`.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Injecting a `+10 mm` thoracic cough during 4D delivery holds gated OAR peak dose `<= D_lim` (`26 Gy`) while ungated OAR peak dose exceeds `D_lim`, verified under `VerifiedEngine::run_verified` with zero dynamic heap allocations across coupled `40 Hz` steps.
- **Positive**: Sculpted PTV/OAR contours round-trip cleanly across `< 4 KiB` stateless share URLs and `SplitHorizontal` `.mws` sessions on both native desktop and `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, while `imrt_oar_sparing` challenge clamping locks canonical circular contours.
- **Neutral**: Full 3D volumetric CT ray-tracing and DICOM network transfers remain out of scope so real-time 2D slice dosimetry sustains 60 FPS in WebAssembly.
