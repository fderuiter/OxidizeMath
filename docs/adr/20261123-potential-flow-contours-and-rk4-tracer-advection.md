# ADR 20261123: Potential Flow Iso-$\psi$ Contours, Stagnation Detection, and `RungeKutta4` Dye Tracer Coupling

## Status
Accepted (extends [ADR 0016](0016-ode-multi-solver-comparative-overlays.md), [ADR 0032](0032-high-contrast-focus-selection-and-raster-tokens.md), [ADR 0035](0035-iterative-ast-glsl-transpiler-and-shader-exports.md), and [ADR 0045](0045-spatial-brush-kit-and-field-sculpting.md))

## Context
[`PotentialFlowField`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/potential_flow.rs) implemented analytic `velocity`, `stream_function`, and `potential` methods across `UniformFlow`, `Source`, `Vortex`, and `Doublet`, while [`FluidParticleSystem`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/solver.rs) implemented `OdeSystem<FlowState>` and `TimeStepper<FlowState>` over [`Euler`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/conservation.rs). However, `PotentialFlowField::stream_function` and `FluidParticleSystem` were not coupled into a verified domain-level contour, stagnation, and Lagrangian dye-tracer pipeline, and `Vortex::stream_function` / `Vortex::potential` used the opposite sign convention from `Vortex::velocity`. Naive grid contouring across `Source` singularities also produces spurious contour bundles across `atan2` branch cuts and inside `TOLERANCE_HIGH` singularity cores.

## Decision
1. **Cauchy-Riemann Parity, Singularity-Aware Iso-$\psi$ Contouring, and Stagnation Detection**:
   - Align `Vortex::stream_function` and `Vortex::potential` signs with `Vortex::velocity` ($u = \partial_y \psi = \partial_x \phi$, $v = -\partial_x \psi = \partial_y \phi$), and add `FlowElement::singularity_center`, `PotentialFlowField::is_near_singularity`, `PotentialFlowField::extract_streamline_segments`, and `PotentialFlowField::detect_stagnation_points` in [`potential_flow.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/potential_flow.rs) backed by `#[verified_engine::verified]` marching-squares and damped Newton-Raphson kernels in [`analysis.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/analysis.rs).
   - Reject marching-squares cells inside `TOLERANCE_HIGH` / cell-core radii and cells where `stream_function` jumps violate Cauchy-Riemann edge continuity across `atan2` branch cuts.
2. **Lagrangian `RungeKutta4` Dye Advection Coupled with `FluidParticleSystem` and `Euler`**:
   - Introduce `PotentialTracerSystem` and `TracerProbeReadout` in [`solver.rs`](../../crates/03_domains/domain_physics/src/physics/fluid_dynamics/solver.rs), implementing `OdeSystem<Vector2<f64>>` and `TimeStepper<Vector2<f64>>` with `RungeKutta4` and clamping speeds near singularity cores.
   - Couple `PotentialTracerSystem::evaluate_probe` with `FluidParticleSystem<'_, Euler>` and Bernoulli's relation to evaluate convective acceleration $(\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u}$, static pressure, and pressure coefficient $C_p$.
3. **Modularized Tactile Wind-Tunnel GUI and WCAG 2.2 AA Reticle Navigation**:
   - Organize `math_explorer_gui/src/tabs/fluid_dynamics/potential_flow/` into `{mod,cache,canvas,controls,interact,presets,shader,tests,wasm_tests}.rs` (`<= 500` lines per file), preserving zero-allocation `PlotPoints::Borrowed` arrow buffers, `SpatialBrushKit` sculpting, `CalloutManager` pins, aerodynamic observables, and GLSL shader export while adding `RungeKutta4` dye streaks, Bernoulli/Euler probe HUD, keyboard reticle nudging with live-region announcements, and Kutta-Joukowski lifting cylinder, Rankine oval, and staggered vortex street presets in [`starter_gallery/fluid_presets.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/starter_gallery/fluid_presets.rs) re-exported by [`starter_gallery.rs`](../../apps/math_explorer_gui/src/starter_gallery.rs).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: `PotentialFlowField::stream_function`, `FluidParticleSystem`, and `conservation::Euler` power live iso-$\psi$ contours, stagnation markers, animated `RungeKutta4` dye streaks, and Bernoulli/convective probe telemetry without `atan2` branch-cut artifacts or duplicated GUI marching-squares solvers.
- **Positive**: Every source file remains within the 500-line ceiling and every verified kernel remains within the 60-statement / 60-line limit, synchronized in [`api_baseline.json`](../../apps/unified_verification/api_baseline.json).
- **Neutral**: Existing `PotentialFlowState` JSON presets remain backward-compatible via `#[serde(default)]` fields while preserving `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` validation.
